# NPCs, Dialogue & Reputation

1. **Add Granny Hazel the herbalist beside the lotus pond** — She sells Qi-restoring teas and trades for raw crafting materials, positioned near Lotus Toad spawns so healers have a resupply stop between pulls.
2. **Add Brother Pine the bamboo cutter at the valley grove** — He trades bamboo used in several of the 10 crafting recipes and offers a daily gather quest that grants Inkbrush Artisan standing.
3. **Add Old Reed the fisherman on the mist-pond dock** — He sells bait consumables that briefly raise drop rates on water-adjacent spirits and runs a fishing-flavored daily turn-in.
4. **Add Aunt Willow the tea-house keeper near spawn** — A rest hub NPC who grants a short out-of-combat Qi regen boost when you sit for tea, giving Talisman Masters a safe recovery spot.
5. **Add the Inkbrush Sage in a hidden meditation alcove** — A lore NPC who reveals discoverable-place hints for the atlas and awards a merit seal for finding all mist-pond-adjacent landmarks.
6. **Add the Cartographer Quill beside the Notice Board** — She unlocks atlas fog-of-war reveals for coin and marks Stone Guardian and Old Whisker regions after you discover them, tying into the discoverable-places system.
7. **Add the Sealsmith Ember near the merit-seal shrine** — He forges and upgrades merit seals from reputation shards, bridging the merit-seal system with the new faction standing.
8. **Add the Beast Warden Rook on the Stone Hound ridge** — He issues bounty quests on specific mobs from the 27-mob roster and sells class-agnostic hunt consumables.
9. **Add the Zither Keeper beneath the mountain silhouettes** — A flavor NPC whose ambient melody changes with day/night and who grants a small temporary buff after you stop to listen.
10. **Add Disciple Ash outside the Stone Guardian arena** — She offers a short pre-boss quest that teaches the Guardian's telegraphed cone/line/beam patterns and three phases before the real attempt.
11. **Add the Poet Sable who wanders the valley paths** — He trades coin for scrolls that unlock lore entries and hint at the rare roaming Old Whisker.
12. **Add the Fortune Teller Pearl in the mist-shrouded corner** — For jade, she previews the next day's daily-board rolls and occasionally points toward Old Whisker's current roamer path.
13. **Add the Courier Swift on the valley road** — A timed delivery quest that pays jade and tests the dodge-roll Qi economy across a route between stalls.
14. **Add the Meditation Master Still-Water at the spawn overlook** — A trainer who permanently raises the 100-point Qi pool's max by a few points per milestone, priced in merit seals and reputation.
15. **Give Elder Lin a class-aware branching dialogue tree** — Extend his 4-step chain so a Sword Cultivator, Talisman Master, and Physician each hear distinct follow-up flavor and a class-specific reward.
16. **Add a keyword-glossary dialogue system** — Players click highlighted terms such as "Qi," "Stone Guardian," or "Old Whisker" for an in-conversation lore snippet instead of one-off exposition dumps.
17. **Add class-skill dialogue checks** — Some responses unlock only for a healer (Physician), a ranged fighter (Talisman Master), or a melee fighter (Sword Cultivator), so replays on other classes differ.
18. **Add reputation-gated dialogue branches** — NPCs greet and trust you differently at each tier, and certain side quests or responses only appear at Friend or higher.
19. **Add a repeatable "ask about the valley" wheel** — Every named NPC offers a lore question wheel that cycles randomized vignettes, so re-talking is rewarded rather than dismissed.
20. **Add dialogue consequences that shift reputation** — Choosing a respectful or dismissive reply moves that NPC's faction standing slightly, giving conversations mechanical weight.
21. **Add a conversation history log panel** — Store the last branch per NPC in the paper-scroll UI so players can re-read a quest hint without re-approaching the NPC.
22. **Add tone tags as brush-pictogram icons** — Mark dialogue warm, wary, urgent, or ominous with small vector icons beside the speaker, aiding players who scan text quickly.
23. **Add text-reveal speed and skip controls** — Tie the typewriter speed to the "reduce motion" accessibility setting and let the dodge/Enter key skip to the full line instantly.
24. **Deliver dialogue through ARIA live regions** — Push each NPC line into an ARIA live region so screen readers announce it, honoring the accessibility priority.
25. **Add a dialogue camera-focus toggle** — On interaction, optionally frame the speaker with ink-outline silhouetting and hide the HUD, disabled automatically under "reduce motion."
26. **Add conditional dialogue for low Qi or low health** — NPCs comment when you arrive depleted or critical and some offer a discounted tea or salve in response.
27. **Introduce four reputation factions** — Cloudmist Wardens (Elder Lin, Gate Warden Wu), Inkbrush Artisans (Artisan Tao, Lantern Keeper Mei), Spirit Watchers, and the Wayfarer's Circle (Peddler Hao).
28. **Define five reputation tiers with a seal motif** — Stranger, Acquaintance, Friend, Respected, and Revered, each rendered as an increasingly vermilion seal stamp beside the faction name.
29. **Reward standing from quests and the daily board** — Completing Elder Lin's chain or Fen's Notice Board dailies grants Warden standing, while crafting and shop purchases raise Artisan standing.
30. **Gate shop inventory by reputation tier** — Higher-tier items in Peddler Hao's and the Sealsmith's lists only appear at Friend or Respected, giving standing concrete purchasing power.
31. **Add reputation shards as a secondary currency** — Earning standing also yields shards spendable at the Sealsmith to forge merit seals, linking reputation to the merit-seal economy.
32. **Add a Spirit Watchers faction for mob interaction** — Defeating or studying the 27 mobs grants Spirit Watcher standing, with spirit-themed cosmetic rewards.
33. **Add reputation milestone titles** — Crossing each tier grants a title such as "Mist-Called" or "Ink-Touched" surfaced in the existing title system.
34. **Add faction-choice friction** — Some quests favor the Wardens or the Spirit Watchers, and a rare dialogue choice shifts standing between the two so allegiance feels real.
35. **Add a reputation overview panel** — Show all four factions, current tier, and next-tier progress with vermilion seal stamps and focus-ring-visible rows.
36. **Add reputation-based greeting variants** — NPCs switch from "Stranger" to "Friend" address and add a small bow emote at Respected, readable in both text and animation.
37. **Add a faction emissary for each of the four factions** — Each emissary explains that faction's ethos and rewards in dialogue, acting as a discoverable entry point.
38. **Add a "word of mouth" reputation spread** — High Warden standing makes unrelated vendors occasionally discount small purchases, reinforcing the valley as a living community.
39. **Add the Dodge Master at the training yard** — A trainer who shaves fractions off the dodge roll's 1.5s cooldown through repeatable dodge-course challenges.
40. **Add a per-class mentor NPC** — Sword Cultivator, Talisman Master, and Physician each get a mentor selling skill-modifying upgrades (cooldown or cost tweaks) at the 20-level milestones.
41. **Add the Bagmaker to expand the 30-slot bag** — She sells incremental bag slots for coin plus a reputation tier, growing inventory in small affordable steps.
42. **Add a rarity-reforge service** — A vendor re-rolls or upgrades a gear piece's rarity across the 4 tiers for coin and jade, giving duplicate gear a sink.
43. **Add a crafting trainer for the 10 recipes** — Artisan Tao teaches each of the 10 crafting recipes progressively instead of all being visible from the start.
44. **Add a title registrar service** — A single NPC lets you swap earned merit titles and preview them on a paper-scroll backdrop before equipping.
45. **Add the Jadebroker as a coin/jade exchange** — A vendor who converts between coin and jade at a fluctuating daily rate, adding a soft economy layer to the wallet.
46. **Add a mist-ferry fast-travel service** — For jade, a ferry NPC moves you between the spawn overlook, the pond dock, and the Stone Guardian approach within the single zone.
47. **Add vendor greeting emotes** — Vendors bow, wave, or offer tea as an emote before their shop opens, with animations disabled under "reduce motion."
48. **Add contextual idle lines by location** — NPCs near the pond mention Lotus Toad croaks and those near the ridge mention Stone Hound howls, grounding flavor in the real zone layout.
49. **Add time-of-day idle lines** — The same NPC shifts from "The mist is thin at dawn" to "Lanterns out, mind the foxes" at night, keyed to the day/night clock.
50. **Add weather-aware idle lines** — When the valley mist thickens, NPCs remark on visibility and warn of rarer spirit activity, tying flavor to the fog-heavy aesthetic.
51. **Add proximity greeting barks** — Walking within a few units of a friendly NPC triggers a short one-line greeting with a soft chime hook, without interrupting movement.
52. **Add player-action reaction lines** — NPCs quip when you dodge-roll near them or return bloodied from the Stone Guardian, making the world feel observant.
53. **Add rare-mob callout lines** — NPCs murmur about "Old Whisker" sightings, and the Fortune Teller can be paid to point toward the current roamer zone.
54. **Add boss-warning lines** — Before your first Stone Guardian attempt, Disciple Ash and the Beast Warden describe its slams and phases, and after enrage they warn of the damage spike.
55. **Add class-recognition flavor** — Villagers react differently to a Physician (grateful) versus a Talisman Master (wary of flashy talismans), adding identity beyond combat.

57. **Add a "gossip" rumor pool refreshed by the daily board** — Each day a random rumor spreads among villagers hinting at a bonus spawn or discounted item, rewarding players who listen.
58. **Expand Peddler Hao's shop with class-starter gear** — He stocks level-appropriate weapons and armor for all three classes across the 4 rarities so early gear is purchasable, not only crafted.
59. **Add Qi elixirs to the shop** — Consumables that instantly restore a chunk of the 100-point Qi pool or briefly boost the 10/s regen, priced in coin with jade upgrades.
