# Events & Live Ops

1. **Lantern Festival: Mei's Wicks Return** — A spring festival that revives Mei's "Wicks for the Lanterns" side quest as a zone-wide version, where paper lanterns drift down Cloudmist Valley's misty river and players light them in sequence for a limited-time seal.

2. **Lantern Festival: Six-POI Lantern Trail** — Scatter six festival lanterns at the six atlas POIs (Hermit's Rest, Old Waystone, Serpent's Rest, Fox Fire Hollow, Hound Ridge Cairn, Cloudmist Overlook), turning the fog-of-war atlas into a timed scavenger hunt through the 200x200 valley.

3. **Lantern Festival: Vermilion Sky Dye** — Tint the sky and fog toward vermilion and gold during the festival window, staying inside the Ink and Vermilion palette while visually marking the season on the layered-mountain backdrop.

4. **Lantern Festival: Riddle Lanterns** — Each POI lantern carries an English-only riddle hinting at the next POI's name, so the trail reads as a paper-scroll puzzle rather than a GPS chase, with no hanzi anywhere.

5. **Lantern Festival: Hao's Festival Wisp** — Peddler Hao sells a festival-only "Festival Wisp" consumable that temporarily brightens your surroundings in the heavy fog, a single-season utility that never outlives the event and never touches combat.

6. **Lantern Festival: Paper Crane Release** — A festival emote that launches a paper crane from the plaza, reusing the ambient crane models as a shared social beat with no combat effect and no Qi cost.

7. **Mid-Autumn: Cloudmist Overlook Moonrise** — A fall festival centered on the Cloudmist Overlook POI where players gather to watch a full moon rise over the layered mountains, earning a "Moon Viewing" title for attending during the window.

8. **Mid-Autumn: Moon-Touched Mob Drops** — During Mid-Autumn, Bamboo and Fox Spirits drop a limited "Moon Petal" collectible, deepening the normal grind loop with a seasonal drop that requires no new mob art.

9. **Mid-Autumn: Toad Moon Jelly** — Lotus Toad Wardens drop a "Moon Jelly" variant collectible during the festival, tying the passive-mob pond loop to the seasonal economy so pond farmers get a festival reason to stay at the stone bridge.

10. **Mid-Autumn: Festival Tea at Hermit's Rest** — The Hermit's Rest POI serves festival tea during the event, a light lore interaction that rewards a sip emote and a small Qi-restore buff, honoring the "warm tea" lore already recorded in the atlas.

11. **Mid-Autumn: Moon Rabbit Spirit** — A seasonal non-combat "Moon Rabbit" spirit hops between POIs during the festival, discoverable via the minimap for a discovery seal, echoing the Jade Serpent's pond habitat with a benign counterpart.

12. **Seasonal: Shared Festival Calendar** — Fen's notice board shows both festivals on a dated scroll (Lantern in spring, Mid-Autumn in fall), so players see active, upcoming, and retired windows in one paper ledger.

13. **Seasonal: Non-Grind Festival Seals** — Festival collectibles are earnable only through normal kills, POI visits, and quests (never jade or payments), honoring the no-payments scope and keeping seasons accessible to all three classes.

14. **Seasonal: Festival Catch-Up Window** — Retired festival cosmetics and titles return in a short catch-up window the following season, so a missed Lantern Festival lantern set is re-earnable rather than permanently locked out.

15. **World Event: Mist Surge** — A periodic event that thickens the fog to near-zero visibility and spawns waves of Bamboo and Fox Spirits across the valley, scaling rewards to the number of players who tag each wave.

16. **World Event: Stone Guardian Siege** — A scheduled event where the Stone Guardian leaves its shrine and marches toward the spawn plaza at 0,-80, requiring zone-wide players to whittle its three phases before it reaches spawn and enrages.

17. **World Event: Lotus Pond Bloom** — A timed event where the pond blooms and Lotus Toad Wardens surface in a frenzy with bonus drops, drawing players to the stone bridge for a shared farm of the passive mob.

18. **World Event: Jade Serpent Migration** — A periodic event where Jade Serpents migrate from Serpent's Rest across the valley, letting multiple players share poison-cleanse duty and drop credit on the 4-second poison bite.

19. **World Event: Stone Hound Stampede** — A pack event where several three-hound Stone Hound packs converge on one hill, testing pack-pull aggro management at zone scale.

20. **World Event: Old Whisker's Rampage** — A rare event that forces Old Whisker to spawn early and roam, broadcasting the existing gold zone-wide line so the whole valley converges for shared credit on the Silver Tail Cloak chase.

21. **World Event: Contribution Tiers** — Score shared-event participation from the `damage` and `heal` events the server already emits, granting tiered coin, jade, and merit rewards so a Physician's Spring Rain counts as much as a Sword Cultivator's strike.

22. **World Event: Shared Telegraph Broadcasts** — During world events, broadcast boss-grade cone/line/beam telegraphs to every participant via the existing `telegraph` event so all players dodge the same ground danger together.

23. **World Event: Communal Claim Chest** — End a world event with a plaza reward chest where each participant claims a scaled reward into their 30-slot bag and wallet, reusing the `loot` and `wallet` messages.

24. **World Event: Fen's Event Ledger** — Show a temporary top-contributor ledger at Fen's board during events, styled as paper with gold names for top scorers and class pictogram icons per the icon rules.

25. **World Event: Rotation Countdown** — A System-channel countdown before each world event ("The mist thickens in 2 minutes") reusing the `sysAll` channel, so players can rally without a static schedule page.

26. **World Event: Reduced-Motion Telegraphs** — During dense world events, telegraphs render with a static high-contrast outline option when reduce-motion is on, keeping the photosensitive-safe rule under dozens of simultaneous effects.

27. **Limited Quest: Elder Lin's Festival Dispatch** — A seasonal re-skin of the Elder Lin chain's first step that asks players to purge festival-tinged Bamboo Spirits during a window, reusing the tutorial quest flow with a seasonal objective.

28. **Limited Quest: Wu's Lantern Errand** — A limited Wu side quest that tasks players with delivering a paper lantern to each of the six POIs before the festival ends, a timed traversal challenge through the fog.

29. **Limited Quest: Tao's Seasonal Recipe** — A window-limited recipe from Artisan Tao that consumes festival drops (Moon Petal, Moon Jelly) to craft a cosmetic festival gear skin, adding a seasonal entry to the 10-recipe loop.

30. **Limited Quest: Hao's Festival Stock Run** — A timed quest to gather festival drops for Peddler Hao in exchange for a festival-only consumable, so the shop gets a seasonal reason to spend coin.

31. **Limited Quest: Mei's Moonlight Request** — A limited Mei quest to light lanterns at the Cloudmist Overlook on a festival night, awarding a "Festival Light-Keeper" title.

32. **Limited Quest: Fen's Event Bounty** — A timed bounty from Fen that asks players to hunt a festival-buffed Old Whisker or Stone Hound pack, feeding the daily board's have/need counters with a seasonal target.

33. **Limited Quest: Class-Tailored Festival Tasks** — Each festival offers one class-specific task (Sword Cultivator tanks a lantern-guard mob, Talisman Master kites Fox Spirits, Physician cleanses festival poison), so all three kits get a seasonal role.

34. **Limited Quest: FOMO-Free Journal** — Retired limited quests stay listed in the journal as completed or missed with their season name, so the quest log never shows a permanent, unexplained gap.

35. **Limited Quest: Expiry Countdown on the Scroll** — Active limited quests show an ink-fade countdown in the journal and on Fen's board, so players see exactly when a window closes without checking an external clock.

36. **Limited Quest: Seasonal Narrative Beat** — A short festival journal entry ties each season to the Hollow Sect breadcrumbs already seeded in the atlas lore, so events advance the story rather than sitting on top of it.

37. **Daily Login: Streak Calendar** — A 7-day login streak page on the paper-scroll UI awarding coin, jade, and a Healing Tonic, mirroring the existing 7-day daily-board streak but keyed to real-time logins.

38. **Daily Login: Day-7 Jade** — The seventh consecutive login grants jade, extending the existing three legitimate jade sources (boss kills, Elder Lin chain, daily board) with a fourth that still requires daily presence.

39. **Daily Login: Class-Appropriate Consumables** — Daily login grants consumables that match your `cls` (a Talisman Master gets a Qi-restore tonic, a Physician gets a Lotus Antidote), so the reward is always useful to the kit.

40. **Daily Login: Streak-Safe Catch-Up** — A missed day pauses rather than resets the streak with a small grace token, so a player who skips one day doesn't lose a month of progress over a single offline day.

41. **Daily Login: Server-Day Alignment** — The login streak is computed on the authoritative server clock so it survives client restarts and the Electron launcher's cache, never keyed to the local browser timezone.

42. **Daily Login: No-Pay Fast-Forward** — Streak days are never purchasable or recoverable with jade, honoring the no-payments scope and keeping the calendar a pure attendance reward.

43. **Daily Login: Reward Inbox to Bag** — Daily login rewards route through a small inbox that auto-deposits into the 30-slot bag, warning when the bag is full so coin and tonics are never silently lost.

44. **Daily Login: ARIA Calendar Readout** — The login calendar is keyboard-navigable with focus rings and an ARIA live region announcing "Day 3 reward claimed: 50 coin," per the accessibility rules.

45. **Battle Pass: Valley Season Track** — A free season track of 50 levels earned purely by playing (kills, quests, boss clears), with no paid tier, fitting the no-payments scope while giving a long-horizon progression.

46. **Battle Pass: Merit-Seal Currency** — Season levels award merit points that feed the existing merit seals/titles system, so the battle pass deepens the completionist loop rather than inventing a new currency.

47. **Battle Pass: Cosmetic-Only Top Rewards** — The season track's top rewards are transmog skins, titles, and pets only, never stats, so a season never invalidates the lv20 power cap.

48. **Battle Pass: Ink Guardian Set** — A season finale grants a full "Ink Guardian" transmog set (weapon, head, body, boots, charm, pendant) tinted to the Ink and Vermilion palette, completing the 6-slot wardrobe visually.

49. **Battle Pass: Class-Pictogram Emblems** — Mid-track rewards include per-class pictogram emblems for the nameplate, so each of the three classes gets a visible season badge without new art assets.

50. **Battle Pass: Season Quest Milestones** — Season levels tie to the existing quests (Elder Lin's chain and the Wu/Tao/Hao/Mei/Fen side quests) as one-time XP, so a fresh character naturally progresses the season by playing the story.

51. **Battle Pass: Daily-Board Season Sync** — Completing Fen's daily board tasks also advances the season track, so the two progression loops reinforce each other instead of competing for playtime.

52. **Battle Pass: Season-Aware Ledger** — Show season level and next reward on the paper-scroll journal alongside the merit percentage tracker, so season progress reads as part of the completionist number.

53. **Battle Pass: Reduce-Motion Season Reveal** — Season reward reveal animations degrade to a static ink stamp when reduce-motion is on, honoring the accessibility rules during the level-up moment.

54. **Battle Pass: Season Length by Real Weeks** — Each season runs a fixed number of real-world weeks (not in-game hours) with a visible end date on Fen's board, so players can plan the season's grind.

55. **Twitch Drops: Watch-to-Earn Seal** — A Twitch Drops campaign that grants a "Viewer's Seal" merit seal to accounts that watch a partnered stream for the threshold, wired through the existing launcher token identity rather than a full account system.

56. **Twitch Drops: Streamer Title** — Partners who stream the game earn a "Valley Storyteller" title that renders after their name, so streamers have an in-game identity beyond the drop campaign.

57. **Twitch Drops: Drops Claim via Code** — Drops redeem through a claim code typed in the launcher/browser settings, matching the existing name+password/token flow and avoiding a separate OAuth account system.

58. **Twitch Drops: Cosmetic Fox Spirit Pet** — A drop-only Fox Spirit cub pet (echoing the cosmetic non-combat follower) is the headline Twitch reward, a cosmetic chase that never touches combat balance.

59. **Twitch Drops: Drop-Proof Bag Delivery** — Claimed drop rewards route to the same inbox-to-bag path as daily login, so a full 30-slot bag triggers a warning rather than a silent loss.

60. **Twitch Drops: No-Jade Drops** — Twitch drops grant cosmetics and titles only, never coin or jade, so stream viewership cannot farm the wallet's three legitimate currency sources.

61. **Twitch Drops: Campaign Window on Fen's Board** — Fen's notice board shows the active drop campaign, its end date, and the claim-code entry field, keeping the whole flow inside the paper-scroll UI.

62. **Twitch Drops: Anti-Dupe Claim Guard** — The server records claimed drop codes against the token so a code can't be redeemed twice, a lightweight guard needing no anti-cheat infrastructure.

63. **Community Challenge: Valley-Wide Bamboo Purge** — A server-wide tally where all players' Bamboo Spirit kills count toward a shared goal, unlocking a zone-wide reward when the total is met.

64. **Community Challenge: Shared Progress Bar** — Fen's board shows an ink-bar percentage for the active community challenge, updating from the server's authoritative kill counters so the whole valley watches the same number.

65. **Community Challenge: Milestone Chests** — Crossing community thresholds (e.g., 10,000 Bamboo Spirits) unlocks a plaza claim chest for every participant, using the communal chest + wallet reward path.

66. **Community Challenge: Tiered Participation Rewards** — Players who contribute above a personal minimum get the milestone reward, so the challenge is communal but still requires each player to actually participate.

67. **Community Challenge: Boss-Slayer Cooperative** — A challenge to collectively defeat the Stone Guardian a set number of times in a window, with a global tally and a "Guardian Breaker" community seal at completion.

68. **Community Challenge: Rare-Hunt Convergence** — A challenge to collectively down Old Whisker a set number of times, rallying the zone on his 20-minute respawn cadence.

69. **Community Challenge: Crafting Drive** — A challenge to collectively craft items with Tao's 10 recipes, rewarding crafters for contributing to a shared total rather than hoarding materials.

70. **Community Challenge: Poison-Cleanse Marathon** — A challenge to cleanse Jade Serpent poison a collective number of times via Purifying Light or Lotus Antidote, spotlighting the Physician's role in a shared goal.

71. **Community Challenge: Weekly Rotation** — Community challenges rotate weekly alongside the daily board, so the shared goal changes (purge, boss, rare, craft) and no single loop stagnates.

72. **Community Challenge: SysAll Progress Pings** — The server emits `sysAll` progress lines at 25/50/75/100% ("The valley has purged 75% of the Bamboo surge!"), reusing the Old Whisker announcement channel for community hype.

73. **Rotating Modifier: Daily Zone Affix** — Each real day the valley gains one rotating affix (e.g., "Mobs regenerate 15% HP" or "Dodge roll costs 20 Qi"), shown on Fen's board so every day's grind feels distinct.

74. **Rotating Modifier: Boss Affix Pool** — The Stone Guardian rolls a random affix per fight (e.g., enrage comes earlier, beams rotate faster), so the 3-phase fight gains replay variety without new boss art.

75. **Rotating Modifier: Qi-Drought Day** — A modifier that halves the 10/s Qi regen to 5/s, forcing all three classes to manage the 100-point pool and 15-Qi dodge more carefully for one day.

76. **Rotating Modifier: Telegraph-Speed Day** — A modifier that speeds up cone/line/beam telegraph windups, so dodge-roll timing (1.5s cd) becomes the day's skill check across the valley.

77. **Rotating Modifier: Mob-Pack Aggro Day** — A modifier that widens mob aggro radius so packs pull from farther away, changing how Sword Cultivators engage and Talisman Masters kite for a day.

78. **Rotating Modifier: Modifier Journal Log** — Each day's active modifier is logged in the journal with a one-line ink description, so players can look back at which affixes shaped their runs.

79. **Rotating Modifier: Modifier-Safe Accessibility** — Rotating modifiers never trigger flashing or high-frequency strobes; any visual change honors the photosensitive-safe and high-contrast rules, degrading to static outlines with reduce-motion.

80. **Rotating Modifier: Modifier Reward Bonus** — Playing under an active modifier grants a small coin or merit bonus to the relevant activity, so the rotating challenge rewards engagement rather than just making the game harder.
