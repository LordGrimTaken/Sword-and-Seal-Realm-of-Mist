# Gear & Equipment

1. **Add a two-ring equipment row** — Unlock a ring slot at level 8 and a second at level 16 so a Qi-regen ring and a dodge-cost ring can stack without crowding the existing 6 slots; rings render as thin jade and vermilion bands on the procedural ink-wash hands.

2. **Add an artifact slot driven by merit seals** — A single artifact slot displays the player's highest earned merit seal as a floating lacquer tag over the shoulder; completing Elder Lin's 4-step chain grants the first seal so the slot teaches itself through a quest instead of a menu.

3. **Add a class off-hand slot with one passive each** — Sword Cultivator gets a scabbard that widens the melee parry window, Talisman Master a brush-holder that grants one extra talisman charge, Physician a herb satchel that adds heal-over-time ticks; each off-hand carries one class-tuned passive rather than raw stats.

4. **Add a cloak slot with a paper-scroll silhouette** — Cloaks drape a procedural ink-wash cloth that trails into the layered depth fog and recedes against the mountain silhouettes; the slot grants a mist-themed proc such as post-dodge damage reduction.

5. **Add a still cloak variant for reduce-motion and photosensitive-safe players** — Give cloaks a "still" variant with no flutter animation so players using reduce-motion or photosensitive-safe mode can wear the slot without animated cloth.

6. **Add a talisman pouch off-hand for the Talisman Master** — The pouch holds crafted single-use talismans drawn from the 10 crafting recipes and lets ranged skills consume a stored charge instead of Qi, adding a gear-driven resource layer to the ranged rotation.

7. **Add a herb satchel that feeds a shared bandage pool for the Physician** — The satchel stores field bandages that scale with the Physician's heal skills, making the off-hand a loadout choice between burst healing and sustained regen.

8. **Make the Sword Cultivator's scabbard modify the dodge roll** — A scabbard reduces the 15-Qi dodge cost to 12 while the 1.5s cooldown holds, turning the slot into a real mobility decision for melee instead of a stat stick.

9. **Add a belt slot for a lacquered gourd Qi reservoir** — A belt with a small procedural gourd raises the 100-point Qi cap by a flat amount so late-game rotations can bank one extra skill per cycle before the 10/s regen tops them off.

10. **Add a 3-piece Cloudmist Wanderer set** — Three Cloudmist Valley-themed pieces grant a stacking mist buff that grows with each distinct mob tagged from the 27-mob roster, rewarding the open-world loop over single-target burst.

11. **Add a 5-piece Stone Guardian slayer set** — Five pieces from the Stone Guardian boss grant reduced damage during its cone, line, and beam telegraphs, explicitly keying the bonus to reading the boss's three phases rather than flat defense.

12. **Add a 2-piece Jade Serpent set** — Two pieces convert a portion of the dodge-roll Qi cost into a small self-heal, giving the melee Sword Cultivator a sustain option tied to the existing 15-Qi dodge.

13. **Add a Lotus Toad recovery set for the Physician** — The set grants Qi-free healing ticks whenever the Physician stays inside heal range of a low-health ally, promoting the pocket-healer role instead of passive stats.

14. **Add a Fox Spirit burst set for the Talisman Master** — The set makes the third ranged skill within a short window deal bonus damage, mirroring the Fox Spirit mob's feint-and-strike pattern in the bestiary.

15. **Add a Bamboo Spirit dodge set** — Wearing three pieces shortens the dodge roll's 1.5s cooldown to 1.2s, a readable set bonus players can feel immediately in combat.

16. **Show set progress as vermilion seal corners on the paper-scroll sheet** — In the gear UI, each equipped set piece lights a vermilion seal corner on its item card so completion is legible without scanning tooltip text, which also helps colour-assist and high-contrast players.

17. **Tie one set bonus to the atlas discoverable-place count** — A Cartographer set whose bonus scales with the number of atlas places discovered, so exploration-minded players earn gear power from the discoverable-places system.

18. **Add an imbuing bench that spends jade for a temporary paper-charm glow** — Imbue a weapon with a mist or vermilion charm that tints its toon-shaded ink outline and lasts one play session, giving the wallet's jade a sink beyond the shop.

19. **Add permanent enchant scrolls as side-quest rewards** — Gate Warden Wu, Artisan Tao, and Peddler Hao each award a one-time enchant scroll on side-quest completion, so enchanting is discovered through existing NPCs rather than a menu dump.

20. **Enchant a weapon with Siphon to refund Qi on kill** — A weapon enchant that returns a few Qi points when a mob dies, letting the Sword Cultivator chain dodge rolls through Bamboo Spirit packs.

21. **Enchant gloves with Sure Grip to smooth the dodge cost under latency** — A glove enchant that makes the 15-Qi dodge roll's Qi deduction predictable across the 20Hz tick, a concrete quality-of-life enchant for headless-tested combat.

22. **Add a Vermilion Mark enchant that recolours the cel-shade outline** — Enchanted gear renders its ink outline in vermilion instead of ink-soft, a purely visual reward that respects the Ink and Vermilion palette and stays legible in high contrast.

23. **Imbue a piece with a mist aura that fades with durability** — The mist aura's intensity decays as durability drops, a non-flashing visual wear indicator that doubles as a reduce-motion-friendly status cue.

24. **Enchant boots with Lotus Step for a post-dodge speed burst** — After a dodge roll, a brief movement bonus lets melee close the gap that the Stone Guardian's line telegraph opens up.

25. **Add a reroll enchant that trades jade for a new minor affix** — Spend jade at Artisan Tao's bench to reroll one enchant affix, giving the wallet's jade currency a repeatable gear sink without any monetization.

26. **Add socket slots that appear as brush-stroke recesses on the item card** — Sockets render as small empty ink-stroke circles that fill with a jade, gold, or vermilion dot when a gem is set, so socket count is visible at a glance without text.

27. **Add a Qi Core gem that raises the 100-point cap** — A socketable gem adding +10 max Qi creates build tension between raw stats and a larger skill-and-dodge budget.

28. **Add a Mist gem granting post-dodge stealth** — A socketed mist gem makes the player briefly fade into the depth fog after a dodge roll, a defensive socket option that leans on the game's fog aesthetic.

29. **Add a Vermilion gem granting a damage proc on the third hit** — A gem that ignites a vermilion ink-burst on every third landed hit, tuned for the Sword Cultivator's melee combo rather than a flat crit chance.

30. **Add a Jade gem granting out-of-combat Qi regen** — A socketed jade gem raises the 10/s regen only while out of combat, encouraging a rhythm of engaging and retreating between mob pulls.

31. **Restrict gems to rarity tiers with a shape-plus-colour legend** — Each rarity tier allows a matching gem tier, and the socket legend uses shape (circle, triangle, square) alongside colour so colour-blind players can read tiers without colour alone.
