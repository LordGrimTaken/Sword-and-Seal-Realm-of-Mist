# Crafting & Gathering

1. **Add Alchemy as a dedicated profession** — Give the Physician (and any class) a cauldron recipe set that turns Serpent Gall and Toad Jelly into new combat tonics, so the two "valuable to alchemists" drops gain a real craft path beyond selling to Peddler Hao.

2. **Brew a Qi Replenishing Tonic** — A crafted tonic that restores a chunk of the 100-point Qi pool instead of health, letting a Sword Cultivator bank extra 15-Qi dodge rolls mid-fight without waiting on the 10/s regen.

3. **Add tiered antidotes against the valley's poisons** — Let alchemy craft antidotes that cure Jade Serpent poison ticks faster and clear the Stone Guardian's future debuffs, so the Physician has a craftable answer for each poison source rather than the single Lotus Antidote.

4. **Brew a Serpent Gall Elixir for a crit window** — A short-duration crit tonic made from the rare Serpent Gall drop, giving the Talisman Master a burst-aligned consumable on the shared 15s potion cooldown.

5. **Add Cooking as a buff profession** — Cook simple field meals from Bamboo Fiber and Toad Jelly that grant out-of-combat regen or move speed, a light food system that speeds the valley loop without adding a hunger bar.

6. **Give Cooking a class-tuned dish per class** — A Sword Cultivator meal for melee damage, a Talisman Master meal for Qi regen, and a Physician meal for heal power, so cooking becomes a preparation step that respects each class's four skills.

7. **Add Talisman Inscription as a tradeable consumable craft** — Let the Talisman Master inscribe single-use paper talismans that echo the class's four ranged skills, turning ranged skills into a consumable other players can buy or receive.

8. **Inscribe a single-use ranged talisman for melee players** — A Sword Cultivator can carry an inscribed Fire Talisman to answer a Lotus Toad Warden's long line spit from a distance, filling the melee class's ranged gap without a respec.

9. **Grind Toad Jelly into talisman ink** — An inscription recipe that converts Toad Jelly into ink, giving the Lotus Toad Warden drop a dedicated sink and tying inscription to the pond-side mob.

10. **Re-inscribe the Hollow Seal Fragment into repeatable wards** — Extend the "Mending the Seal" quest so the fragment can be bound into further warding charms, making the Old Whisker drop a repeatable craft rather than one-time quest fodder.

11. **Add the Formation Array profession** — A craftable ground array item that lays a short zone reusing the Stone Guardian's circle-telegraph rendering to slow or damage mobs, turning the boss's own telegraph language into a player tool.

12. **Craft mob-specific arrays** — A "Repelling Array" against Stone Hound packs and a "Serpent Ward" that blunts Jade Serpent lunges, so arrays counter named valley threats instead of being generic damage zones.

13. **Craft a Stone Guardian mitigation array** — A one-use array that reduces slam and scatter damage inside its radius, helping a Physician-and-melee party survive the boss's phase-2 telegraphs.

14. **Gate the strongest arrays behind jade** — Require jade for top-tier arrays so the wallet's rare currency feeds a premium craft sink beyond Peddler Hao's shop.

15. **Add Beast Taming as a non-combat profession** — Let players tame a passive cub from the 27-mob roster (a Bamboo Spirit or Fox Spirit cub) that gathers materials while it follows, giving a companion without combat power creep.

16. **Make a tamed cub auto-loot nearby materials** — A tamed cub gathers Bamboo Fiber or Fox Tail within a small radius over time, easing the 10-recipe grind without a full pet combat system.

17. **Require a crafted "Spirit Bell" to tame a cub** — A recipe that converts Hound Fang and Jade Scale into a bell used to befriend a cub, so taming flows through Artisan Tao's crafting loop rather than a menu.

18. **Add a beast pen taming station near the fox hills** — Place a pen close to the Fox Spirit spawns so taming lives beside the mobs it uses, and cubs are fed at a physical station instead of an abstract menu.

19. **Expand the 10-recipe table with Serpent Gall and Toad Jelly sinks** — Add recipes that consume the currently underused Serpent Gall and Toad Jelly, so every one of the six materials has a deterministic craft rather than only a sell price.

20. **Add a Fox-Fur Hood headpiece recipe** — A fine head-slot craft from Fox Tail that complements the existing Bamboo Weave Hat, giving the head slot a second craftable option.

21. **Add a craftable jade pendant** — A mid-level fine pendant recipe from Jade Scale and Serpent Gall gives the pendant slot a craftable early option before the rare Cloudmist Pendant and epic Guardian's Heart.

22. **Add a Greater Healing Tonic recipe** — Craft a stronger tonic from a Healing Tonic plus Serpent Gall for a bigger heal on the same 15s potion cooldown, an upgrade recipe that reuses the existing consumable as an ingredient.

23. **Add a boss-material recipe chain** — A multi-step recipe that turns the Guardian Core (a guaranteed Stone Guardian drop) into additional consumables or cosmetics, so repeated boss kills have a sink beyond the single Guardian's Heart craft.

24. **Lean on multi-output recipes to respect the 30-slot bag** — Favour recipes that yield quantity 2+ like the existing tonic, so gathering and crafting stay bag-friendly within the 30-slot inventory cap.

25. **Add physical gathering nodes to Cloudmist Valley** — Spawn bamboo thickets, fox dens, and serpent nests at fixed coordinates in the 200x200 zone so Bamboo Fiber, Fox Tail, and Jade Scale can be gathered rather than only dropped from mobs.

26. **Add a Mist Herb node for a new alchemy material** — A node that only appears in the deepest fog layers, so gathering teaches the layered-fog navigation and feeds the new alchemy recipes.

27. **Make gathering a brush-stroke minigame** — Replace instant clicks with a short ink-brush stroke the player traces (reusing the calligraphy rendering), where skill improves yield, with a one-button fallback for reduce-motion players.

28. **Time the fox-den gather to the mob's cone-bite rhythm** — A Fox Spirit den gather that requires waiting for the still moment between the mob's cone-bite telegraphs, so gathering reuses combat-timing skill instead of a timer.

29. **Drive node respawn on the authoritative 20Hz tick** — Give each node a respawn timer owned by the server so a farmed bamboo thicket regrows predictably and headless tests can verify the timing deterministically.

30. **Add a rare Golden Bamboo node** — A low-chance golden bamboo node that yields extra Bamboo Fiber with a gold (#c9a227) glow, a rare-world reward that also teaches the palette's gold accent.

31. **Deplete nodes with a still, high-contrast fade** — Harvested nodes fade to mist (#8fa3ad) until respawn, and the depletion state is a non-animated change so colour-assist and photosensitive-safe players read it clearly.

32. **Place a themed gather node at each of the six POIs** — Put a node at Hermit's Rest, Fox Fire Hollow, and the other atlas places so discovering a place leads to a gathering payoff.

33. **Grant a small XP trickle for gathering** — Each node harvested awards a modest quest-XP amount so pure gatherers can progress toward the 20-level cap without only killing mobs.

34. **Add crafted-item quality tiers (Rough/Fine/Superb)** — Give every recipe output a quality roll that nudges its stats within its rarity, so two Houndfang Jians differ and crafting has a repeatable chase.

35. **Tie quality to the gathering minigame result** — A perfect brush-stroke harvest yields higher-quality material, which in turn raises the crafted item's quality, linking gathering skill to craft output.

36. **Show quality as ink-swirl count on the paper-scroll card** — Quality renders as one to three ink swirls on the item card, echoing the rarity ink-swirls already in the gear UI so it reads without numbers.

37. **Let quality affect the sell price to Peddler Hao** — A Superb crafted item sells for more coin, making quality a soft-economy decision rather than purely a stat roll.

38. **Add a Masterwork quality proc at max profession mastery** — At high profession mastery, crafting can yield a Masterwork with a third cel-shading tone, a visual prestige signal inside the existing toon pipeline.

39. **Keep every quality tier usable** — Quality only nudges stats and never locks a player out, so a poor roll still yields usable gear and new players are never punished by RNG.

40. **Turn surplus Bamboo Fiber into wicks at Lantern Keeper Mei** — A repeatable turn-in that converts excess Bamboo Fiber into coin or a small merit, extending Mei's "Wicks for the Lanterns" quest into an ongoing sink.

41. **Add a bulk-sell with diminishing returns at Peddler Hao** — Hao pays less per unit past a daily threshold so the six materials have a controlled coin sink and cannot inflate the wallet.

42. **Add a shrine offering sink at the Old Waystone** — Donate materials at the Old Waystone POI for a temporary buff or a merit seal, a lore-friendly material sink outside the shop.

43. **Add a fixed-rate material exchange at Artisan Tao** — Let players trade between the six materials (e.g. three Bamboo Fiber for one Toad Jelly) so an unlucky drop streak never dead-ends a recipe.

44. **Post material bounties on the daily board** — Notice Board Fen posts "collect X" dailies that consume materials on turn-in, pacing the material sink through the existing daily board.

45. **Add profession mastery ranks 1–5 per profession** — Track crafted items per profession (smithing, alchemy, inscription, arrays, taming, cooking) and grant ranks that lower coin/jade cost or raise output quantity.

46. **Extend the "Apprentice Smith" merit seal into a profession family** — Add merit seals for each profession (e.g. "Alchemist's Nose", "Inkscribe") reusing the existing counter-driven merit system in data.ts.

47. **Unlock recipe tiers through mastery** — Reaching rank 3 in a profession reveals new recipes at Artisan Tao, so the recipe table grows with mastery instead of level alone.

48. **Let smithing mastery cut the dodge cost on crafted boots** — A high-mastery smithing perk that reduces the 15-Qi dodge cost on boots the player crafts, tying profession mastery back to the core movement economy.

49. **Show mastery as an ink-brush progress bar** — Render mastery as a hand-drawn ink stroke filling toward the next rank, matching the existing paper-scroll progress style in the journal.

50. **Grant a profession title at mastery rank 5** — Award titles like "Master Alchemist" or "Array Sage" through the existing title system, so profession progression feeds the titles ledger.

51. **Persist profession mastery in node:sqlite** — Add a profession-mastery table so ranks survive WebGL client reloads and Electron launcher sessions, mirroring the existing content persistence.

52. **Craftable paper lanterns** — Extend Lantern Keeper Mei's Paper Lantern quest item into a craftable cosmetic lantern the character carries, reusing the lantern VFX already in the plaza.

53. **Craftable ink-wash dye tints** — Recipes that recolour a robe's cel-shade within the Ink and Vermilion palette (ink, jade, vermilion, gold, mist), a cosmetic that never strays outside the design tokens.

54. **Craftable charm danglers for the pendant slot** — Purely visual danglers (a jade tassel, a fox-fur tuft) that restyle the pendant without changing stats, expanding the charm slot's visual variety.

55. **Craftable companion dyes from Beast Taming** — A recipe that tints a tamed cub's outline (ink, jade, or vermilion) so beast companions are visually distinct in the fog.

56. **Keep cosmetics out of the 30-slot bag** — Store cosmetic unlocks in a separate wardrobe, following the existing transmog approach so cosmetics never crowd the 30-slot inventory.

57. **Craftable seasonal lantern tints** — A Lantern Keeper Mei-gated recipe that shifts the plaza lantern colour to gold or jade for a session, a cosmetic that visibly changes the shared world.

58. **Give Artisan Tao a per-profession station menu** — Split Tao's single craft list into Smithing, Alchemy, and Inscription tabs so the growing recipe table does not become one long scroll.

59. **Add a brush desk station for Inscription at Hermit's Rest** — A second crafting site in the world so inscription feels distinct from Tao's forge and gives the Hermit's Rest POI a concrete purpose.

60. **Add a campfire cooking station near the pond bridge** — A cooking station at the pond area where players grill gathered materials, anchored to the existing pond-and-bridge prop.

61. **Require proximity to stations like the existing NPC services** — Reuse the SVC_RANGE "too far away" check so a player must stand at the cauldron, desk, or fire, matching how craft and buy already gate distance.

62. **Render stations as paper-scroll panels with focus rings** — Give each station its own paper-scroll UI with visible focus rings and ARIA labels so crafting is keyboard and screen-reader reachable.

63. **Add a portable crafting kit consumable** — A one-use kit that opens a limited craft menu anywhere in Cloudmist Valley for an emergency tonic mid-run, consuming itself to stay balanced.

64. **Show recipe materials as vector pictogram icons** — Each recipe row uses the hand-drawn pictogram icons for materials (fiber, tail, scale) instead of text-only, matching the icon language of the gear UI.

65. **Make a failed gather degrade the node, never break it** — A botched brushstroke yields less material and leaves the node depleted, so gathering is forgiving and never punishes a misclick.

66. **Let multiple players harvest the same node** — Allow a party to gather the same bamboo thicket with a small per-player yield so grouped players do not compete for node ownership.

67. **Place the best nodes inside mob aggro range** — Put high-yield nodes within Stone Hound or Fox Spirit aggro so gathering requires clearing or dodging the very mobs that drop the same material.

68. **Add a rare Gall Pool node for Serpent Gall** — A rare pond node that grants Serpent Gall (currently a low 8% drop) so alchemists have a deterministic, if risky, farm path.

69. **Add a Toad Jelly node guarded by Lotus Toad Wardens** — A jelly node at the pond shore where the toads' long line-spit telegraph protects the harvest, tying gathering to the existing combat encounter.

70. **Rotate the daily gather objective through all six materials** — Notice Board Fen cycles which material to gather daily so no node type goes stale and the daily board stays varied.

71. **Reveal node spawns on the minimap after first harvest** — Once a node type is first harvested, its spawn points appear on the client minimap as faint ink marks, aiding navigation through the heavy fog.

72. **Let Superb materials substitute for missing common materials** — A high-quality material can stand in for a missing quantity of a common one, smoothing the bag math when a recipe is one item short.

73. **Add a material-only pouch within the 30-slot cap** — A small material pouch that holds gathered materials separately from gear and consumables so crafting does not fight for bag space.

74. **Add a daily crafting budget for top crafts** — A small per-day crafting budget refilled at reset (and optionally refreshed by the daily board) so the strongest recipes stay paced without a hard stamina bar.

75. **Craft a Fox-Fang Charm and a Toad-Skin Charm** — Two fine charm-slot recipes so the charm slot has more craftable options beyond Jade Charm, Lotus Charm, and Hound-Fang Charm.

76. **Craft an atlas ink that reveals undiscovered POI hints** — An alchemy recipe that briefly marks the nearest undiscovered atlas place on the map, tying crafting to the discoverable-places loop.

77. **Give a tamed cub a profession-themed idle animation** — A tamed cub's idle reflects the player's highest profession (carrying a brush, sniffing herbs), a small cosmetic payoff for profession mastery.

78. **Add a weekly master-craftsman capstone at Artisan Tao** — Once a week Tao offers a special recipe requiring one of each of the six materials plus jade and yielding a rare or epic item, a weekly chase and sink for the 20-level endgame.

79. **Add a gather-and-craft tutorial side quest from Artisan Tao** — A short quest that walks a player through harvesting a node, running the minigame, and crafting one recipe, teaching the loop through the existing NPC chain.

80. **Persist crafted-item quality and mastery in the JSON snapshot** — Extend the authoritative full-state snapshot to carry profession ranks and item quality so headless tests can verify the crafting loop deterministically.
