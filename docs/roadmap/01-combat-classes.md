# Combat & Class Design

1. **Add the Blade Dancer as the fourth class** — a melee skirmisher with a Momentum meter (0–100) that fills on basic slashes and drains between fights, so its damage floor is lower than the Sword Cultivator's but its sustained ceiling is higher; it gives Cloudmist Valley a high-mobility melee that can kite Stone Hound packs instead of facetanking them.
2. **Blade Dancer skill 1 — Whirlwind Slash** — a 3-Qi melee spin that hits every mob in a 4-unit radius and grants one Momentum per target hit, mirroring Flowing Slash's cost but rewarding players who wade into Bamboo Spirit packs.
3. **Blade Dancer skill 2 — Shadow Step** — a 10-Qi, 6-unit dash toward a target that deals a low-power hit and grants brief i-frames, giving the class a movement tool that does not consume the shared 15-Qi dodge roll and therefore extends its dodging budget against the Stone Guardian's rotating beam.
4. **Blade Dancer skill 3 — Crescent Arc** — a 20-Qi, 8-second-cooldown cone strike whose power scales with current Momentum, converting the meter into a telegraphed burst that rewards building stacks before a Fox Spirit's cone bite forces a roll.
5. **Blade Dancer skill 4 — Momentum Surge** — a 30-Qi self-buff that locks the Momentum meter at full for 5 seconds and raises crit chance, so the Blade Dancer can front-load damage during the Stone Guardian's phase-2 scatter telegraphs without losing the ramp.
6. **Add the Formation Master as a fifth class** — a zone-control support that drops persistent ground formations (modeled as circle telegraphs in the existing protocol) instead of direct heals, giving groups a way to hold the pond bridge against Jade Serpent lunges.
7. **Formation Master skill 1 — Warding Array** — a 12-Qi circle at the cursor that slows any mob inside by 40% for 6 seconds, reusing the `slow` field already in `SkillData` and giving the class a tool to blunt Stone Hound packs.
8. **Formation Master skill 2 — Ember Array** — a 20-Qi ground circle that pulses fire damage every second for 5 seconds, the game's first persistent AoE, tuned so a well-placed array can clear the low-tier Bamboo Spirit grove while the caster kites.
9. **Formation Master skill 3 — Azure Link** — a 15-Qi tether between two allied players within 16 units that redirects 20% of damage from the lower-HP ally to the higher-HP one, giving a Physician partner more breathing room during the Guardian's slam phase.
10. **Formation Master skill 4 — Grand Seal** — a 40-Qi, 30-second-cooldown area that silences mob windups inside it for 3 seconds, briefly preventing Lotus Toad spit and Jade Serpent lunges so the party can safely burst a target.
11. **Add the Alchemist as a sixth class** — a DoT-and-utility thrower whose skills are lobbed projectiles that apply burn and poison stacks, filling the gap between the Physician's heals and the Talisman Master's burst with a specialist that softens high-HP rares like Old Whisker.
12. **Alchemist skill 1 — Ember Flask** — a 4-Qi lobbed projectile that ignites a target for a burn DoT, giving the class a cheap spammable that parallels Fire Talisman but trades burst for persistent pressure.
13. **Alchemist skill 2 — Venom Cloud** — a 15-Qi point-target cloud that poisons mobs inside for several seconds, reusing the exact poison-tick pipeline already used by the Jade Serpent and thus requiring no new networking beyond the existing `damage` events.
14. **Alchemist skill 3 — Iron Vapour** — a 12-Qi self-centered mist that reduces incoming damage by 25% for 4 seconds, giving the squishy thrower the survivability it needs while it waits for its DoTs to tick.
15. **Alchemist skill 4 — Lethal Decoction** — a 30-Qi single-target detonation that consumes all of a target's burn and poison stacks at once and deals their combined remaining damage instantly, rewarding the class for stacking DoTs on Old Whisker before the kill window.
16. **Sword Cultivator skill — Reverse Edge** — a 5-Qi, 1.5-second parry stance that negates the next melee hit within 0.8 seconds and refunds 10 Qi on success, giving the melee class a counter tool against Fox Spirit and Stone Hound cone bites instead of always spending the 15-Qi dodge.
17. **Sword Cultivator skill — Cyclone Cleave** — a 15-Qi spinning slash that hits all mobs around the player and briefly roots them, a crowd-control option so the Sword Cultivator can peel Stone Hound packs off a Physician ally.
18. **Talisman Master skill — Gale Talisman** — a 12-Qi cone that pushes mobs back 4 units, giving the ranged class a knockback to reset a Jade Serpent that has coiled to lunge, without adding a new damage type.
19. **Talisman Master skill — Mirror Talisman** — a 20-Qi self-buff that reflects 30% of incoming ranged damage back at the attacker for 4 seconds, a defensive answer to the Lotus Toad Warden's long line spit.
20. **Physician skill — Needle Barrage** — a 10-Qi burst of three auto-target needles that each apply a small bleed, giving the support class a damage option that still leaves room in its kit for Spring Rain and Meridian Surge.
21. **Physician skill — Breath of Recovery** — a 25-Qi channeled group heal over 3 seconds that also removes one poison or burn stack per tick, turning the Physician into the counter to Jade Serpent poison for a party fighting near the pond.
22. **Give every class a swappable fourth slot** — let players choose 4 of 6 class skills for their hotbar, so a Sword Cultivator can trade Qi Guard for Reverse Edge and a Physician can swap Needle Barrage for Breath of Recovery, without changing the 1–4 cast protocol or the HUD.
23. **Add per-skill ranks from 1 to 5** — each rank adds a flat or percentage increase to power, radius, or duration, purchased with jade at Gate Warden Wu, so the 20-level cap gains horizontal progression without new XP sources.
24. **Gate rank unlocks behind class quests** — require finishing Elder Lin's chain or a side quest before a skill can be ranked past 2, tying power to the story and the existing quest system rather than a bare vendor.
25. **Add a three-branch talent tree per class** — one branch per identity (Sword: burst / sustain / peel; Talisman: AoE / kiting / control; Physician: direct heal / regen / damage), with each node referencing existing skills by name.
26. **Make talent points come from level-ups and merit seals** — award one point per level plus bonuses from Combat-category merit seals like Pack Breaker, so the merit system already in `data.ts` feeds the talent economy.
27. **Add a shared "Cultivation" talent row** — nodes that improve Qi (regen, pool, dodge cost) are available to every class, so build variety includes a mobility/utility axis independent of the class's damage identity.
28. **Let talent choices be re-specified at Elder Lin for coin** — a one-click reset that scales with level, so players are not locked out of experimenting at the 20-level cap; no new currency is introduced.
29. **Show rank and talent effects in the existing skill tooltip** — extend `skilltips.ts` to render the rank-adjusted power, radius, and Qi cost so the HUD reflects the tuned numbers the server actually uses.
30. **Add a "signature" passive unlocked at level 10 per class** — a single strong class-defining trait (Sword Cultivator's first crit in a fight is guaranteed, Talisman Master's bolts pierce one target, Physician's heals refund Qi when the target is below 30% HP) that makes the second half of the 1–20 climb feel distinct.
31. **Add a Sword Cultivator Rage meter** — generated by taking and dealing damage and spent by Heaven-Splitting Strike, so the melee class has a second, slower resource on top of the shared jade Qi pool.
32. **Add a Talisman Master Focus meter** — building Focus over 2 seconds of not moving and breaking on movement, rewarding the ranged class for holding still against Lotus Toad spit and making kiting a real decision.
33. **Add a Physician Vitality reserve** — a second small pool that only heals draw from, so a Physician can keep healing allies after the shared Qi pool is drained by dodges, without touching the global Qi economy.
34. **Add Blade Dancer Momentum stacks (0–10)** — each stack adds 3% attack and decays after 6 seconds out of combat, converting the class's fast rotation into a visible ramp that the HUD shows as a second bar.
35. **Add Formation Master Array Charges (max 3)** — charges that refill on a timer and gate how many formations can be active at once, so zone control is bounded and the class cannot blanket the boss arena.
36. **Add an Alchemist Reagent gauge** — Flask skills consume reagents that build from dealing DoT ticks, closing the loop so the class must keep poison/burn uptime to fuel its detonations.
37. **Add a shared out-of-combat buff, "Cultivator's Calm"** — after the existing 5-second out-of-combat delay, the first skill cast costs 0 Qi, smoothing the lull between Stone Hound packs and reducing the "empty Qi" frustration.
38. **Add an exhaustion state at zero Qi** — when the Qi pool hits 0, dodge is disabled and a 2-second "Qi Drained" slow applies, making total Qi depletion a punishable moment rather than a soft number; the HUD flashes the jade bar with a pattern cue for colour-assist users.
39. **Add a burn status** — a fire DoT that ticks every 1 second for 3 ticks and is applied by Fire Talisman and Alchemist flasks, reusing the Jade Serpent poison pipeline so no new network path is required.
40. **Add a freeze status** — Frost Seal upgrades from a 40% slow to a full freeze (mob cannot act or move) for 1 second on the third successive application, making the Talisman Master's kiting skill a real crowd control.
41. **Add a bleed status** — a physical DoT that ignores a mob's damage reduction and stacks up to 3 times, giving the Sword Cultivator a sustained option against the high-HP Stone Guardian where burst is wasted.
42. **Add a stun status** — a short (0.8s) hard disable applied by blunt melee strikes and some boss-phase transitions, tuned so it interrupts the Stone Guardian's telegraphed slam only if timed precisely.
43. **Add a root status** — prevents movement but not actions, applied by Cyclone Cleave and Formation Master arrays, so kiting classes can pin a lunging Jade Serpent in place.
44. **Add a curse status** — an inverted-HP debuff that reduces the target's healing received by 40%, applied by the Hollow Sect mask mobs to punish groups that rely on a single Physician's Spring Rain.
45. **Add a guard-break status** — a short debuff that strips the target's damage-reduction and Qi Guard bonuses, letting an Alchemist's Lethal Decoction open up the Stone Guardian's enraged phase for the party's burst.
46. **Give each status a single shared duration/tick model** — define burn, poison, and bleed in one `StatusEffect` table in `data.ts` with `ticks` and `every`, so the poison code in `combat.ts` generalizes instead of accumulating special cases.
47. **Show status icons on the target frame with a timer ring** — small brush pictogram icons (flame, snowflake, needle) on the mob's health plate, so a Talisman Master can see exactly when Frost Seal's freeze will land without guessing.
48. **Make statuses visible to colour-blind players by shape, not colour** — each status icon carries a distinct silhouette (flame, drop, shard) and a pattern fill, satisfying the accessibility rule that colour is never the only cue.
49. **Let statuses interact in a fixed priority order** — freeze overrides slow, burn and poison stack separately but bleed refreshes, documented in a tooltip so players can plan combos instead of discovering rules by accident.
50. **Add a cleanse skill interaction** — Purifying Light and Breath of Recovery remove one burn or poison stack per cast, and the Lotus Antidote already cures poison, giving the Physician a clear counter to the Jade Serpent's bite.
51. **Give mobs statuses too** — the Stone Guardian gains a "Rune Fracture" debuff when a phase transitions, and rare mobs like Old Whisker can be slowed, so player crowd control is useful against the valley's named threats rather than only trash.
52. **Add a block for the Sword Cultivator** — holding a new block key reduces incoming melee damage by 60% while standing still but drains Qi per second, an alternative to the 15-Qi dodge that favors facetanking Stone Hound packs.
53. **Add a perfect parry window** — blocking within the last 0.2 seconds of a mob's windup negates the hit and refunds Qi, so skilled players can farm Stone Hound cone bites for free instead of rolling away.
54. **Add a counterattack after a successful parry** — the next Flowing Slash within 2 seconds crits, giving the Sword Cultivator a reward loop that the `crit` field in the damage event already supports.
55. **Add a Physician blink instead of a roll** — replace the shared dodge with a short-range teleport for the Physician, keeping the same 15-Qi cost and cooldown but matching the class's support fantasy and giving it a tool to escape the Guardian's beam.
56. **Let the dodge roll cancel attack windups** — allow a roll to interrupt the player's own cast animation and grant i-frames immediately, so dodging the Stone Guardian's 1.8-second slam is responsive even if the player was mid-swing.
57. **Track dodge success as a combat stat** — count "Dodged!" events and show them as a per-session metric in the journal, then add a merit seal "Crane's Grace" for dodging 50 telegraphs, rewarding mastery of the roll.
58. **Add a block-dodge Qi trade-off tooltip** — the HUD Dodge slot explains that blocking costs Qi per second while dodging costs a flat 15, so new players understand the two defensive economies without reading patch notes.
59. **Make parry and counter visually distinct from dodge** — a white paper flash for a parry versus the existing roll VFX, keeping the ink-wash palette (ink outline, paper-white accent) and avoiding any colour-only cue.
60. **Add a second weapon line per class** — Sword Cultivator gains a heavy greatsword variant (slower, higher power) and a light blade (faster, crit-oriented), each with the same 4 skills but different implicit modifiers.
61. **Give Talisman Masters a fan and a brush weapon** — the brush favors single-target bolt damage while the fan spreads AoE radius, so the same four skills play differently based on the equipped weapon in the `weapon` gear slot.
62. **Give Physicians a staff and a needle case** — the staff boosts heal power and Spring Rain's radius while the needle case boosts damage and crit, letting the class lean toward pure support or a hybrid damage build.
63. **Add weapon-specific proc effects to rare and epic items** — an epic Cloudpiercer Jian gains a 5% chance for its next Flowing Slash to cost 0 Qi, while an epic brush refunds Qi on a killing blow, making top-tier weapons feel distinct beyond flat attack.
64. **Show weapon identity in the existing gear tooltip** — `itemtips.ts` lists the weapon's implicit (speed, AoE radius, heal power) alongside its rarity pips, so players can compare a Houndfang Jian to a Cloudpiercer Jian at a glance.
65. **Tie weapon choice to build, not level** — make all weapon variants available from level 1 through Artisan Tao's recipes, so a level-8 player can craft a greatsword, fan, or staff with the same materials rather than being forced into one archetype.
66. **Add a three-hit basic-attack chain for the Sword Cultivator** — a Slash → Slash → Thrust sequence where the third hit deals bonus damage and generates extra Qi, so melee combat has a rhythm beyond waiting for Flowing Slash's 1-second cooldown.
67. **Add a cast-order combo for the Talisman Master** — Fire Talisman → Frost Seal on the same target detonates the freeze for bonus damage, giving the ranged class a sequence to chase beyond the 1.2-second Fire cooldown.
68. **Add a Physician "resonance" combo** — landing Jade Needle three times on one mob marks it, and the next Spring Rain cast also detonates the mark for damage, giving the healer a rewarding attack loop while soloing Bamboo Spirits.
69. **Add a cross-class combo** — a Sword Cultivator's Heaven-Splitting Strike on a mob that a Talisman Master froze within the last 2 seconds triggers "Shatter" for bonus damage, incentivizing mixed parties against Stone Hounds.
70. **Show combo progress in the HUD** — a small ink-brush chain counter above the hotbar advances as a combo builds and flashes gold on completion, so players learn the sequences without a written guide.
71. **Make combos interruptible but not punishing** — failing a combo simply resets the counter with no penalty, so the system rewards skill without frustrating players who must roll away from a Stone Guardian slam mid-chain.
72. **Support three archetypes per class** — define each class's damage, control, and sustain builds in the talent tree (Sword burst vs. peel, Talisman AoE vs. kite, Physician direct vs. regen) so a group can assemble distinct roles.
73. **Let gear stats pivot a build** — because `atk`, `crit`, `hp`, and `def` are already summed from the 6 slots, add a few crit- and hp-focused charm/pendant options so a Physician can trade defense for attack without a class change.
74. **Add a build share string** — encode talent choices and equipped gear into a short text token that can be pasted into chat or the settings export, so players can copy a "Stone Guardian tank" build from a friend.
75. **Add a build snapshot in the journal** — store the player's current talents, weapon, and stats as a readable page, giving a place to plan and compare builds with the merit seals and titles already listed there.
76. **Balance builds around the Qi economy, not raw damage** — every build must respect the 10/s regen and 15-Qi dodge, so a "maximum damage" build still cannot out-sustain a build that invests in Qi talents; document this on the Cultivation talent row.
77. **Move all combat numbers into `data.ts` tables** — extend the existing `SkillData`/`MobDef` pattern so rank coefficients, status durations, and talent modifiers live in one data file the server reads, keeping balance changes to a single edit.
78. **Add a combat log and tuning harness** — a TEST_MODE-only dump of damage-per-skill, uptime, and Qi spent, so the team can verify that the 10.5–11 Qi/s rotation target still holds after adding ranks and talents.
79. **Set time-to-kill targets per mob tier** — state explicit TTK goals (a same-level Bamboo Spirit dies in ~6 seconds solo, a Stone Hound pack in ~20 seconds with a partner) and tune power so the 1–20 curve stays at the documented ~1.5-hour cap.
80. **Tune the boss DPS check against enrage** — balance the Stone Guardian's 150-second enrage (1.5x damage) against expected level-8 party damage so a competent group clears it before enrage but a solo level-8 player must kite, and expose the enrage timer on the boss plate.
