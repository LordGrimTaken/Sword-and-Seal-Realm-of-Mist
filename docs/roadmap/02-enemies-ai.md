# Enemies & Mob AI

1. **Add a Mistborne yaoguai demon family** — Introduce corrupted-forest demons (a Verdant Spitter, Toad-Warted Brute, and Thornvine Creeper) that share the jade #4fa58b corruption tint so players read them at a glance against the mist #8fa3ad fog.
2. **Build yaoguai from procedural ink-brush geometry** — Model each demon as a procedurally assembled brush-stroke body with 2-3 tone cel shading and vermilion #c8372d eyes, keeping the no-external-assets rule while making silhouettes readable through depth fog.
3. **Give yaoguai a Qi-sapping ranged globe** — Add a lobbed projectile that drains 15 Qi on hit so Sword Cultivators must budget the 15-Qi dodge roll against the 100-point pool, punishing roll-spam during melee gap-closing.
4. **Escalate Lotus Toads into an armored brute yaoguai** — Add a Toad-Warted Brute that extends the existing Lotus Toad line with a damage-reduction shell, requiring Talisman Masters to kite and break its back plating with focused ranged hits.
5. **Make one yaoguai drain Qi on every melee hit** — Add a Leech-Scaled demon whose strikes siphon 8 Qi, directly countering the 10/s regen and forcing players to weave in Physician Qi restoration or retreat.
6. **Let a yaoguai phase-shift when a packmate dies** — When an allied demon falls, trigger a brief enrage/phase tint (ink #1a1a1f to vermilion #c8372d) that grants one empowered slam, so pack clears force a sequencing choice instead of free AoE spam.
7. **Add translucent Paper Wisp restless spirits** — Spawn paper #f2ead8 wisp silhouettes with ink outlines and no solid fill, so the toon/cel shader and fog depth-sort them as clearly spectral against receding mountain silhouettes.
8. **Fix spirits that float over the pond's missing collision** — Since server-side structure collision doesn't exist, add a per-mob hover/terrain flag so pond spirits glide over water intentionally rather than exposing the walk-through-buildings bug to players.
9. **Tie a Lantern-Bound Soul to Lantern Keeper Mei** — Add a restless spirit that haunts the lanterns Mei tends and only aggros after a player completes her side quest, weaving mob behavior into the existing quest chain rather than a detached spawn.
10. **Add a Fog-Woven Shade that materializes at range** — Spawn a spirit that is invisible beyond ~20 units and only fades in as the mist thickens, teaching players to watch fog disturbances near the spawn point at (0,-80) instead of relying on nameplates.
11. **Make shrine-fleeing spirits leash to holy ground** — When a spirit drops below 25% HP it retreats toward the nearest shrine marker and de-aggros at its threshold, giving a natural reset point that prevents kiting into safe zones.
12. **Add a spirit that phases to dodge melee but not Talisman fire** — Give one restless spirit a short ghost-phase dodge that Sword Cultivators cannot hit but ranged Talisman Master bolts pierce, creating a class-composition reason to group.
13. **Add the hopping revenant (jiangshi) family with fixed-hop telegraphs** — Give each revenant a rigid, straight-armed hop with a fixed hop distance and a 0.6s pre-hop crouch, so players learn to sidestep on the telegraph rather than react to the impact.
14. **Let revenants resurrect once unless sealed by a Talisman Master** — A defeated revenant re-stands once at 30% HP unless a Talisman Master lands a specific sealing skill, handing that class a unique mob-specific utility no other class has.
15. **Spawn a Graveward Hopper only at dusk** — Gate one revenant spawn to in-game dusk near the abandoned shrine, tying spawn logic to a day/night tint shift so the world feels alive without a second zone.
16. **Make a revenant steal coin on hit** — Add a Coffin-Toll revenant that takes 5 coin per successful hit and drops a pooled wallet-cache on death, pressuring the coin/jade wallet and rewarding precise dodging.
17. **Add the Mistwood Bandit humanoid family with gear drops** — Introduce cloth-and-leather bandits (Cutpurse, Arbalest, Scout) whose drops feed the 10 crafting recipes, closing the loop between combat and the crafting bench.
18. **Give bandit archers a telegraphed line shot** — Mirror the Stone Guardian's line telegraph on a bandit arbalest bolt so players who learned boss tells can transfer that skill to open-world adds, lowering the learning cliff.
19. **Spawn a three-man bandit patrol as a pack** — Combine one melee tank, one archer, and one scout that flees to summon a reinforcement wave, testing a solo player's target priority before they face grouped content.
20. **Add a named bandit lieutenant with a 4-rarity drop** — Give a fixed-spawn lieutenant a unique vault key or rare-rarity gear piece across the 6 gear slots, making named mobs the reliable source of the top rarity tier.
21. **Route bandit patrols along fixed 200x200 waypoints** — Assign each patrol a loop of waypoint nodes within Cloudmist Valley's 200x200 bounds, pausing at props like the shop and pond so patrols feel placed rather than wandering at random.
22. **Give Bamboo Spirits an ambush clone in the groves** — Let a Bamboo Spirit split into two illusory copies when it enters a bamboo grove, with only the vermilion-eyed original dealing real damage, rewarding observation over tab-targeting.
23. **Teach packs to flank the highest-threat target** — When three or more packmates share a target, have the two outermost mobs strafe toward the target's flanks, spreading players out of the cone/beam safe spots they learned on the Stone Guardian.
24. **Make a scout flee to summon reinforcements** — When a bandit scout drops below 40% HP it breaks aggro and sprints to a nearby camp node; killing it within 4 seconds prevents a 2-mob reinforcement wave, adding a chase-and-intercept check.
25. **Let Old Whisker summon cub spirits when cornered** — Add a summon phase to the rare roaming Old Whisker where it calls two small spirit cubs, so the existing rare gets a distinct mechanic instead of being a stat-stick upgrade.
26. **Give Stone Hounds a bury-and-emerge phase-shift** — Let a Stone Hound submerge into the ground for 2 seconds and re-emerge under a telegraphed ring, porting the Stone Guardian's telegraphed-slam grammar down to a common mob.
27. **Defend a territory den with escalating aggro** — Mark a den node where a pack's aggro radius doubles and reinforcements stream in, so entering a lair is a deliberate risk rather than a longer leash.
28. **Pause patrols at buildings and the pond** — Because server-side structure collision is absent, script patrol nodes to halt and inspect at the shop and pond edges so mobs don't visually clip through the very structures players can currently walk through.
29. **Ambush from the fog line at the zone edge** — Place a Mist-Lurker that waits beyond the visible fog line and lunges only when a player crosses a trigger, using the heavy depth fog as a concealment mechanic instead of a backdrop.
30. **Shift behavior by time of day** — Make Bamboo Spirits docile at dawn and feral beasts nocturnal, with a single server-side day-phase flag toggling spawn tables and aggro ranges rather than scripting each mob.
31. **Add a stalk-crouch predator that ambushes the rear** — Give a feral beast a slow crouch-walk that avoids line-of-sight aggro until it's directly behind the player, punishing players who never pan the camera.
32. **Buff a pack when its alpha survives** — When a pack's alpha mob is alive, grant nearby packmates a small attack-speed buff and a visible jade #4fa58b aura, teaching target priority by making the alpha the clear first kill.
33. **Flee-to-heal on low HP with a retreat windup** — Let wounded beasts retreat to a den to regenerate out of combat, but telegraph the retreat so players can burst it down before the heal, converting a stall into a DPS check.
34. **Summon adds during an enrage window** — During a named mob's enrage phase, spawn 2-3 low-HP adds that must be cleared or they buff the boss, extending the Stone Guardian's phase grammar to named open-world encounters.
35. **Mark territory with scent nodes that trigger intruder aggro** — Place invisible scent markers a beast deposits along its patrol; a player crossing a marked node sets the whole pack on alert, making traversal a stealth-lite layer.
36. **Build an affix system on the four palette accents** — Add Vermilion (bleed on hit), Jade (out-of-combat regen), Mist (slow aura), and Gold (bonus coin) affixes that tint a mob's ink outline so the affix stays legible through colour-assist and high-contrast settings.
37. **Make elites carry an affix plus an HP multiplier** — Promote a normal spawn to elite by rolling one affix and a 2.5x HP multiplier, so elites feel like upgraded versions of the 27 existing mobs rather than new bodies.
38. **Give named mobs a fixed spawn and one unique skill** — Each named mob has a deterministic spawn point and a single signature skill (a vault-leap, a coin-scatter, a spirit-tether) so players can learn and plan around a repeatable challenge.
39. **Expand the rare pool beyond Old Whisker** — Add two more roaming rares (a Cinder-Eyed Fox and a Mossback Serpent) that share Old Whisker's roaming AI but carry different affix tables, so rare-hunting has variety within the single zone.
40. **Colour-code affix telegraphs to the palette** — Render each affix's tell in its palette accent (bleed rings in vermilion #c8372d, slow fields in mist #8fa3ad, regen glow in jade #4fa58b) so colour-blind players can also read the icon overlaid on each telegraph.
41. **Drop a merit seal or title from each named mob** — Award a one-time merit seal or title on first kill of a named mob, wiring combat achievements into the existing merit-seal/title system instead of a parallel progression.
42. **Draw an elite crown as a brush pictogram icon** — Mark elites and named mobs with a small hand-drawn brush crown icon in the UI and over the mob's head, matching the vector brush pictogram language used across the paper-scroll UI.
43. **Gate affix combos by area level** — Restrict two-affix elites to the deeper, higher-level parts of Cloudmist Valley so the affix pool scales with the 20-level cap instead of throwing endgame modifiers at a fresh spawn.
44. **Announce rare spawns with a fog ripple** — Instead of a chat broadcast, ripple the fog and play a subtle ink-swirl on the minimap when a rare roams near, keeping discovery diegetic and photosensitive-safe (no full-screen flash).
45. **Give elites a server-authoritative respawn timer** — Track elite and named respawns on the authoritative Node server (not client-side), so their 5-10 minute respawn windows survive single-process restarts and remain fair.
46. **Tie a named mob to the Elder Lin quest chain** — Make a named yaoguai the capstone of Elder Lin's 4-step chain, so the quest boss is a persistent, repeatable named spawn rather than a one-shot scripted encounter.
47. **Scale respawn rate by local player density** — Raise the respawn rate of Bamboo and Fox Spirits in a sub-area when 3+ players farm it, preventing spawn-starvation while avoiding a flood for solo players.
48. **Cluster spawns around active quest objectives** — Bias spawn points toward whichever of Elder Lin's, Gate Warden Wu's, or the Notice Board's objectives are currently active for nearby players, so mobs appear where quest text points.
49. **Grant a short respawn grace period against camping** — When a mob respawns, give it 1.5 seconds of non-aggro and a brief spawn-ink dissolve so players cannot pre-aim spawn points for free first hits.
50. **Seed each instance's spawn table deterministically** — Derive the instance's spawn RNG from a server-generated seed at boot, so the single zone/process has reproducible mob placement for testing and balancing.
51. **Shift the night spawn table toward spirits and revenants** — Swap daytime Bamboo/Fox Spirit spawns for Paper Wisps and hopping revenants when the day flag flips to night, reusing the same spawn nodes with a different table.
52. **Leash newly-spawned mobs against spawn-camping** — Give fresh mobs a warded flag that ignores players standing within 8 units of the spawn node for 3 seconds, breaking the most abusive camp patterns.
53. **Budget spawn waves per sub-area** — Cap concurrent mobs per sub-area (e.g. 8 in the bamboo groves) and emit replacements in waves, keeping 20Hz server load and the 27-mob roster predictable.
54. **Occlude spawns outside player view** — Only materialize a respawn when no player camera is looking at the node, so mobs never pop in mid-frame in the WebGL client's fogged view.
55. **Maintain a per-class threat table on the server** — Track threat per player with Sword Cultivator damage weighted highest, so the melee class reliably holds aggro in group pulls instead of whoever hit first.
56. **Generate threat for Physician healing** — Add heal-based threat (each HP restored adds threat proportional to the target's current aggro) so Physicians must manage their healing output rather than spam in group fights.
57. **Snap mobs back with a tether on leash break** — When a mob exceeds its leash radius it disengages, returns at sprint speed, and becomes immune for the walk-back, closing the infinite-kite exploit without a hard teleport.
58. **Make Qi expenditure raise local aggro** — Using a skill or dodge roll near an unaggroed mob adds a small threat pulse, so players can't burn the 100-point Qi pool in a mob's blind spot for free.
59. **Enforce line-of-sight through the depth fog** — Require actual line-of-sight (blocked by trees and rock outcrops, not the fog itself) before a ranged mob aggroes, so the mist hides the mob visually without hiding broken targeting.
60. **Add a taunt and a control skill to split threat** — Give Sword Cultivator a short taunt and Talisman Master a root, creating the tank/control split the group-scaling content needs to be interesting.
61. **Decay threat over time for inactive attackers** — Reduce a player's threat toward a target if they deal no damage or healing for 8 seconds, so a kited mob correctly retargets the active threat.
62. **Render the leash as an ink rope tether** — Draw the active leash as a fading ink-brush rope from mob to target when near the boundary, an accessible visual cue (paired with a subtle icon for colour-assist users).
63. **Scale mob HP and damage by group size** — Multiply a mob's HP and outgoing damage by a factor that rises with the number of nearby aggroed players, so a 3-player group doesn't trivialize solo-tuned spawns.
64. **Promote adds to elites at 3+ players** — When three or more players engage a pack, promote one mob to an elite affix variant on the fly, keeping group pulls a step above solo pulls without a separate spawn table.
65. **Balance shared loot by contribution with a fairness floor** — When a group downs a named mob, distribute the guaranteed coin/jade and roll the gear drop per-player with a minimum share, avoiding first-hit-takes-all grief.
66. **Gate group-scaled difficulty behind a Qi-sync check** — Adjust the scaling factor by the party's average level and Qi reserve so a mixed 20-level group doesn't carry fresh players through scaled content.
67. **Add a difficulty tier selector to the Notice Board** — Let a group pick Normal or Hard on a Notice Board Fen bounty that only spawns for 3+ players, reusing the scaling system as an explicit, opt-in challenge.
68. **Scale Stone Guardian phases to the party size** — Add one extra slam per phase and a longer enrage window for larger parties, so the boss's telegraphed-slam grammar remains legible while its numbers scale.
69. **Stage bandit-vs-yaoguai skirmishes in open areas** — Spawn a scripted 3v3 fight between bandits and yaoguai at a few fixed nodes, letting players third-party, pick a side, or sneak past a living world event.
70. **Let feral beasts hunt Bamboo Spirits** — Give predators a prey-targeting AI that chases Bamboo Spirits across the zone, creating cross-mob chases players can follow to loot the loser.
71. **Add a faction reputation for aiding one side** — Track kills-for/against the bandit and yaoguai camps in a hidden reputation value that shifts shop prices or unlocks a side-quest from Peddler Hao.
72. **Spawn an escort bodyguard that aggroes with its charge** — Make a caravan NPC (a Gate Warden Wu patrol) that travels with two guard mobs aggroing anyone who hits the NPC, blending escort logic into the existing side-quest cast.
73. **Make spirits recoil from the Physician's jade ward** — Give the Physician a small jade #4fa58b ward aura that restless spirits path away from, granting that class a niche against spirit packs and encouraging mixed group composition.
74. **Balance predator and prey populations on a timer** — When feral beasts over-hunt Bamboo Spirits, pause their spawns until the prey repopulates, keeping the 27-mob roster's ecosystem stable across long sessions.
75. **Add the pond as a deep-water slow plus spirit ambush** — Make the pond a slow-zone where a Lotus Toad or Paper Wisp surfaces to ambush wading players, turning the collision-less water into a hazard rather than a shortcut.
76. **Drop ambushers from the bamboo canopy** — Place a vine or beast mob that descends from the bamboo canopy onto a player standing still for 2+ seconds, punishing AFK-stops and rewarding moving cover.
77. **Add falling-rock hazards to the Stone Guardian arena** — During phase 3, drop telegraphed rocks at the arena edge that damage mobs and players alike, giving the boss room more environment than a flat dueling circle.
78. **Thicken mist banks into slow-and-hide zones** — Create pockets of dense mist that slow movement and hide mobs entirely, forcing players to use the reduce-motion and high-contrast accessibility cues to navigate.
79. **Add thorn bramble patches that apply bleed** — Place thorn patches that apply a slow bleed dot while stood in, nudging players onto paths and giving the vermilion #c8372d bleed affix an environmental cousin.
80. **Add cliff-edge knockback hazards** — Give knockback-capable mobs and the Stone Guardian a push that can send players off the zone's cliff ledges, making positioning matter against the receding-mountain backdrop.
