# Pets, Mounts & Companions

1. **Seed the companion roster from the valley's existing bestiary** — Let Bamboo Spirit, Fox Spirit, Jade Serpent, Lotus Toad Warden, and Stone Hound be tamed as spirit-beast companions so every mob already in the world doubles as a pet instead of adding a parallel roster.

2. **Give each class a matched starter spirit beast** — Sword Cultivator pairs with a Stone Hound, Talisman Master with a Fox Spirit, and Physician with a Lotus Toad Warden, so the companion reinforces the role rather than shipping a generic starter.

3. **Add an Old Whisker cub as a rare companion drop** — Old Whisker, the Grey Fox, has a small chance to leave a juvenile grey-fox companion whose wide-arc bite echoes the rare's own cone telegraph.

4. **Make the Jade Serpent companion keep its poison bite** — A tamed serpent applies the same periodic-damage poison events as its wild form, scaled to the pet's level, so the mob's identity survives taming.

5. **Make the Bamboo Spirit companion keep its line smash** — The companion inherits the mob's stalk-smash line telegraph at reduced reach, so the pet visibly teaches the very tell players must dodge.

6. **Make the Stone Hound companion a pack-taunter** — The companion's pack instinct lets it pull a single mob's aggro, giving the Physician a tanking pet for solo pulls in the northern foothills.

7. **Make the Lotus Toad Warden companion keep its passive stance** — The companion stays passive until commanded, then spits a long-range line bolt, mirroring the toad's aggro-near-zero behaviour as a support tool.

8. **Map companion rarity to the four gear rarities** — Companions use the same common, fine, rare, and epic tiers as gear, so rarity language and colour coding stay identical across the paper-scroll UI.

9. **Add bestiary cards for each tameable species** — Each spirit beast gets an atlas-style card with an ink-wash portrait and English-only valley lore, extending the discoverable-places atlas to living creatures.

10. **Add a class-affinity bonus to matched pairs** — A companion paired with its matching class gains a small stat edge, so a Physician with a toad reads as a deliberate build rather than a cosmetic choice.

11. **Introduce a craftable Jade Rope for capture** — Artisan Tao crafts a Jade Rope from Hound Fang and Bamboo Fiber; use it on a wounded mob below a health threshold to attempt a tame.

12. **Tie tame chance to the existing level-difference curve** — The tame attempt borrows the xpLevelMult curve so grey mobs (well below the player) tame easily and higher-level mobs resist, reusing a number the game already tunes.

13. **Make the tame attempt a Qi channel** — Channelling the capture drains Qi per second from the 100-point pool, so taming competes with the dodge budget for the same resource instead of being free.

14. **Keep Old Whisker and the Stone Guardian untameable** — The named rare and the boss stay hunt targets only, preserving the "Whisker's Bane" and "Guardian Breaker" merit seals as kill achievements.

15. **Turn a failed tame into a brief mob enrage** — A failed capture enrages the mob instead of showing a flat failure message, reusing the existing aggro and leash numbers so the risk is felt in combat.

16. **Show a receptive state as a vermilion paper-charm ring** — Before a tame attempt the mob shows a vermilion ring to signal it is receptive, readable in colour-assist and high-contrast modes without any text.

17. **Store a captured companion as a sealed charm in the bag** — Until bonded, a captured beast occupies one of the 30 bag slots as a sealed charm, so capture has real inventory pressure alongside materials and gear.

18. **Gate bonding to a ritual at the northern shrine** — A one-time bond at the shrine turns a sealed charm into a live companion, tying the feature to the Stone Guardian quest geography and its northern road.

19. **Capture companions from the same spawn groups as wild mobs** — Companions are tamed from the identical spawn groups their wild counterparts already use, so no separate pet spawn logic or new coordinates are needed.

20. **Add a daily-board tame objective** — A new "Tame 1 spirit beast" daily teaches the mechanic the way the board already teaches kill, loot, and talk objectives, with coin and jade rewards.

21. **Record a lineage on a paper ink-scroll** — Two bonded companions of the same species produce a cub whose stats interpolate the parents' values, written into a lineage scroll in the paper UI.

22. **Use the Guardian Core as a breeding catalyst** — Spending a Guardian Core in breeding pushes the offspring toward rare or epic tier, giving the boss drop a second sink beyond the Guardian's Heart recipe.

23. **Let offspring inherit one parent skill** — A cub inherits one of its two parents' skills plus one random skill, keeping breeding outcomes legible on the pet skill card without a hidden stat table.

24. **Put a real-time cooldown on each breeding pair** — A pair rests for a fixed cooldown before another attempt, preventing idle duplication in the single-process, single-zone server.

25. **Produce a 1-slot spirit egg to incubate** — Breeding yields a spirit egg that takes one bag slot and hatches as you walk the valley, turning ordinary travel into a hatch timer.

26. **Allow Fox Spirit breeding to roll a silver-tail coat** — Breeding two Fox Spirits has a small chance of a silver-tail coat variant, foreshadowing Old Whisker's look without copying the rare's drop.

27. **Accelerate egg incubation through atlas discovery** — A spirit egg hatches faster as you discover the 6 places of interest, linking breeding to the exploration loop instead of a passive clock.

28. **Award a lineage merit seal and title** — Breeding a new generation grants a "Lineage" merit seal with a title, plugging the system into the existing achievement board rather than a separate menu.

29. **Add a Cloudmist pack mule mount** — A procedural ink-wash mule moves faster than walking but cannot dodge-roll, making mounts a travel choice that trades the 1.5s dodge for speed.

30. **Draw mount sprint from the Qi pool** — Riding sprint costs Qi from the same 100-point pool as skills and dodge, so mounts and combat share one resource instead of adding a separate stamina bar.

31. **Dismount on any damage taken** — Taking a hit dismounts the player, so mounts stay a travel tool and never let anyone ride through the Stone Guardian's telegraphed slams.

32. **Tier mount speed by the four rarities** — A common, fine, rare, and epic mount each shortens the crossing time of the 200x200 valley, reusing the gear rarity ladder the player already reads.

33. **Add a Fox Spirit hill-stride mount** — A rare Fox Spirit mount lets the rider pass through Stone Hound pack pulls un-aggroed, but only within the northern foothills, keeping the benefit localized.

34. **Restrict mounts to the plaza stable** — A single stable near spawn (0,-80) is the only mount access point, so travel speed is earned and centralized in the plaza rather than spawned anywhere.

35. **Add mount barding as a cosmetic recolour** — Barding recolours the mount's toon-shade and ink outline within the Ink and Vermilion palette, giving mounts a visual identity without new stats.

36. **Offer a walk-only mount gait for reduce-motion** — Mounts expose a "walk-only" option with no gallop bounce or screen shake, so reduce-motion and photosensitive-safe players keep full access.

37. **Add a recall whistle for stray mounts** — A whistle item recalls a stray mount from anywhere in the zone, safe because the game has no server-side collision to block the recall path.

38. **Make the boss shrine a no-mount zone** — The Stone Guardian's shrine radius disallows mounts, keeping the 3-phase ground-telegraph arena intact for every group that rides up.

39. **Give each class its own flight form** — Sword Cultivators ride a flying sword, Talisman Masters a flying talisman, and Physicians a jade cloud, so flight is class-flavoured rather than a shared mount.

40. **Unlock flight at the level-20 cap** — Flying opens at the level-20 cap, reading as endgame mobility instead of a shortcut that would skip the 1-20 curve and the Stone Guardian.

41. **Cap flight speed to the zone size** — Flight is tuned to cross the 200x200 valley in under a minute and no faster, so the single zone never feels trivial from the air.

42. **Drain Qi continuously while flying** — Sustained flight drains the 100-point pool faster than the 10/s regen refills it, so flight is a sprint the player must land from before the pool empties.

43. **Show a mist-ring low-Qi landing cue** — When Qi is too low to sustain flight a mist-ring landing indicator appears, using a non-flashing cue that is photosensitive-safe.

44. **Reveal serpent spawns from the air** — Flying over the pond gives a top-down view of Jade Serpent spawn points, a concrete exploration reward for reaching the cap.

45. **Force a landing when any class skill is cast** — Casting any of the four class skills while airborne forces a landing, so flight stays travel-only and never becomes a kiting exploit.

46. **Trail paper-charm sparks behind Talisman flight** — The Talisman Master's flight leaves a short trail of vermilion paper-charm sparks, dimmable for photosensitive players and kept within the palette.

47. **Disable flight inside the shrine arena** — Like mounts, flight is blocked around the Stone Guardian so its cone, line, and beam telegraphs remain the test of the fight.

48. **Mirror the source mob's telegraph in pet skills** — A tamed Stone Hound keeps its cone bite and a Bamboo Spirit its line smash, so companions teach the exact tells the player dodges against wild mobs.

49. **Scale companion stats with the owner's level to 20** — Pet HP and damage track the owner's level up to the lv20 cap, so companions stay relevant without a separate pet XP curve.

50. **Give each pet a two-slot action bar** — Each companion carries two active skills and one passive, so it deepens combat without crowding the player's existing 1-4 hotbar.

51. **Charge pet skill Qi to the player pool** — Companion skills draw from the owner's 100-point Qi pool, so ordering a pet attack competes with the player's own dodge and rotation budget.

52. **Add a "Guard" command for the Physician's pet** — Ordering the pet to guard makes it absorb the next single-target hit, a peel tool that leans on the mob aggro system to protect the healer.

53. **Turn the Lotus Toad Warden's spit into an ally heal-over-time** — A tamed toad's jelly-spit becomes a small heal-over-time for allies, mirroring the Physician's Spring Rain identity rather than dealing damage.

54. **Add a "Track" skill for hunting Old Whisker** — A Fox Spirit companion can point toward Old Whisker's respawn direction, turning the 20-minute rare hunt into a guided loop.

55. **Render pet stats as ink brush-stroke bars** — The pet sheet draws HP, Qi cost, and cooldowns as brush-stroke bars on the paper-scroll UI, avoiding a wall of numbers.

56. **Sync pet level to the lowest party member** — Companions in a party scale to the lowest player's level, so a high-level pet cannot carry a friend through the Stone Guardian.

57. **Validate companion casts on the 20Hz server tick** — Pet actions are resolved authoritatively on the same 20Hz tick as player skills, keeping companion combat server-true in the JSON protocol.

58. **Follow AI that respects the no-collision world** — The companion pathfinds toward the owner on the 2D grid without server-side collision, so it visually navigates the shrine and pond without tunnelling.

59. **Leash the companion to the owner's leash distance** — The companion returns to the owner when it exceeds a leash range, mirroring the mob leash numbers already in data.ts.

60. **Assist the owner's current target** — The pet attacks whatever the owner last struck, so it supports the rotation without needing its own targeting UI.

61. **Add hold, passive, and follow stances** — Three stance toggles let a Physician hold the pet back during a careful pull, reusing the mob aggro model for command intent.

62. **Add a "Call" command to peel a mob** — Ordering "call" makes the pet taunt one mob off the healer, using the same pack-pull rules as the wild Stone Hound.

63. **Teach companions to step out of the boss beam** — Pets learn to leave the Stone Guardian's sweeping beam telegraph, so they do not stand in the phase-3 enrage and die to it.

64. **Revive a fallen companion at the plaza after a cooldown** — A downed companion returns to the owner after a short cooldown, never requiring a corpse run across the 200x200 zone.

65. **Idle ink-wash wander animation** — An idle pet wanders a few units and returns, animated with the same procedural toon+ink outline as its wild counterpart.

66. **Ship companions silent by default** — Because audio is unheard and untested, pets launch with no bark or chirp hooks until audio is validated on Windows and macOS, avoiding unverified sound.

67. **Limit coat tints to the Ink and Vermilion palette** — Companions can be tinted paper, mist, jade, gold, or vermilion only, with no colour outside the established six-colour palette.

68. **Add a paper-charm collar cosmetic** — The collar renders as a hanging paper charm carrying a vector pictogram, legible without text for colour-assist players.

69. **Unlock coat variants through merit seals** — Earning "Fox Hunter" or "Pond Purifier" unlocks a matching coat variant for that species' companion, tying cosmetics to the achievement board.

70. **Keep pet names English-only with a character cap** — Player-given pet names are English-only text, consistent with the no-hanzi rule, with a short length cap for the nameplate.

71. **Add a "still companion" toggle for reduce-motion** — The pet can idle perfectly still with no flutter or bobbing, satisfying reduce-motion and photosensitive-safe players without losing the pet.

72. **Add a high-contrast pet outline option** — A toggle thickens the pet's ink outline against the depth fog, matching the existing high-contrast accessibility setting.

73. **Grant a Paper Lantern charm from Mei's quest** — Completing "Wicks for the Lanterns" grants a Paper Lantern charm that hangs from the pet's collar, tying a cosmetic to an existing quest.

74. **Build a single plaza stable** — A procedural stable near spawn (0,-80) is the one place to store, swap, and bond companions and mounts, centralizing the feature in the plaza.

75. **Cap the stable at six companions** — The stable holds up to six bonded companions, leaving the 30-slot bag free for materials, tonics, and gear rather than pet clutter.

76. **Persist stored companions in node:sqlite** — Companions left at the stable are written to the existing SQLite schema, surviving reconnect without growing the live full-state payload.

77. **Serialize only the active companion in snapshots** — To respect the JSON full-state snapshot budget, only the equipped pet is serialized each tick while stored pets live in SQLite.

78. **Make the stable the only mount summon point** — The plaza stable is the only place to summon a stored mount, keeping travel-speed access deliberate and centralized.

79. **Add a stable-keeper NPC with /talk dialog** — A new plaza NPC teaches bonding and storage through the same /talk pattern as Elder Lin and Mei, rather than a menu dump.

80. **Price stable slots in coin and jade** — Storing common and fine companions costs coin while rare and epic slots cost jade, giving the wallet's two currencies distinct uses.
