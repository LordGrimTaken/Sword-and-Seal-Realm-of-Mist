# Bosses & Encounters

1. **Scarlet Mist Fox Matriarch (regional)** — Add a three-tail Fox Spirit boss patrolling the north mist line of Cloudmist Valley whose kit upgrades the base Fox Spirit's dash into a triple-image rush, teaching dodgers to time their 15-Qi roll against overlapping tells. Drops fox-brush crafting mats that feed Artisan Tao's recipe set.
2. **Jade Serpent Sovereign (regional)** — Make a king variant of the Jade Serpent that coils around the pond south-west of spawn, constricting a telegraphed ring so the Talisman Master must kite while the Sword Cultivator breaks the coil. Drops a jade-tier (#4fa58b) gear token keyed to one of the 6 gear slots.
3. **Bamboo Colossus (regional)** — Spawn a colossal Bamboo Spirit whose slams scatter a timed fan of splinter projectiles, testing whether the dodge roll's 1.5s cooldown is enough to weave between gaps. Its bamboo-core drop is a rare crafting component with 4-rarity tiers.
4. **Lotus Toad Overlord (regional)** — Give the Lotus Toad an overlord form that sits on the pond and fires skipping water-glob bullet lines that must be hopped with the dodge roll, reinforcing jump-over-the-gap movement rather than standing in the safe cone.
5. **Stone Hound Alpha (regional)** — Add a pack-leader Stone Hound that calls two Stone Hounds as rotating adds, so solo players learn to burn the alpha before the pack re-forms; the pack kill feeds the daily board's mob-hunt progress.
6. **Old Whisker's White Rival (regional)** — Introduce a rare white fox rival that shares Old Whisker's roam route and only appears when a player holds the Lantern Keeper Mei lit-lantern buff, making the lantern questline a prerequisite for the encounter. First kill unlocks a hidden atlas entry.
7. **Vermilion Warden (regional)** — Station a gate-guardian golem at the pass north of spawn whose vermilion (#c8372d) telegraphed cone is a true one-shot until its ink core is exposed, teaching position-into-the-safe-arc discipline the Stone Guardian slams already hint at.
8. **Mist Wraith (world-roam regional)** — Add a fog-bound wraith that is only visible inside the heavy depth-fog layers, forcing players to read its mist (#8fa3ad) outline instead of a target ring; defeat reveals a discoverable place in the atlas.
9. **Grand Crane Spirit (regional)** — Add a migrating Crane Spirit that lands in Cloudmist Valley on a visible timer, dropping a feather token spendable at Peddler Hao for a cosmetic ink-brush title; it never respawns until the next migration window.

10. **Ink Caverns: Brush Warden (dungeon)** — First dungeon boss that repaints the floor into live ink zones every 25% HP, so the safe floor IS the telegraph and standing still in old safe spots becomes fatal.
11. **Ink Caverns: Ink Well Elemental (dungeon sub-boss)** — A sub-boss that drips ink pools which reduce dodge-roll distance by half when walked through, forcing the group to drag it around the arena's perimeter instead of kiting through the center.
12. **Vermilion Pagoda: Seal Guardian (dungeon)** — A boss whose attacks are hidden until a rotating vermilion seal un-blanks them one arc at a time, rewarding a Talisman Master who watches the seal corner instead of the boss model.
13. **Vermilion Pagoda: Paper Armory Sentinel (dungeon sub-boss)** — A sentinel that summons paper armor plates which must be stripped in order, teaching the add-priority stack before the raid tier reuses it at scale.
14. **Mistgrove Shrine: Lantern Devourer (dungeon)** — A darkness boss that extinguishes the arena into near-black and only re-lights where players carry lanterns, making the Lantern Keeper Mei lantern a mechanically load-bearing quest item rather than flavor.
15. **Mistgrove Shrine: Spirit Calligrapher (dungeon final)** — Final boss that draws living pictogram minions from its brush, each glyph inheriting one of the three class counters, so the group must match glyphs to the right class to clear the wave.
16. **Dungeon Miniboss Gauntlet (dungeon)** — A gauntlet room that chains the Ink Caverns sub-boss into the Pagoda sentinel with no Qi regen reset, so the group budgets its 100-point Qi pool across two fights and 10/s regen matters for pacing.
17. **Dungeon Boss-Rush Floor (dungeon)** — Add an optional rush floor after the final boss that replays all dungeon bosses at +20% speed for a single bonus chest, a repeatable endgame chase for the 20-level cap that avoids new content cost.

18. **Celestial Brush Dragon (raid)** — Eight-player raid boss that paints the arena into a rotating chessboard of ink and paper squares, requiring the raid to stand on paper (#f2ead8) tiles that a beam will not erase.
19. **The Nine-Tailed Ink Fox (raid)** — Raid boss that splits into nine fox images each with a distinct 1-2-3 telegraph order, forcing eight players plus a tank swap to call out which image is real from its ink outline weight.
20. **Vermilion Phoenix (raid)** — A raid boss whose enrage turns the arena into a radial vermilion bloom pattern, converting its soft-enrage into a burn phase where the Physician must sustain through ramp instead of simply resetting.
21. **Stone Sage of the Mountain (raid)** — Raid boss that reuses the Stone Guardian's cone/line/beam language but layers all three at once with a stagger timer, the natural graduation from the valley's solo telegraphed boss to coordinated raid movement.
22. **The Pale Mist Emperor (raid final)** — Final raid boss that periodically whites out the screen into a fog layer and relocates its silhouette to a random mountain backdrop, so the raid relocates by reading receding silhouette depth cues.
23. **Spirit Calligrapher Adds Phase (raid)** — A raid intermission where the boss pauses to draw adds that channel a heal onto the boss until interrupted, giving the Sword Cultivator a defined interrupt job and the Talisman Master a burst-window call.
24. **Raid Intermission: Ink Lotus Bloom (raid)** — Insert a no-damage intermission where the boss hides and the raid must activate three lotus bloom seals in order, a pure coordination check that also lets accessibility reduce-motion toggle simplify the bloom flash.
25. **Raid Final Transition: Paper Rebirth (raid)** — When the raid boss drops to 1% it tears itself into paper strips that reform with full HP once, gating the real kill behind a second enrage timer so the first "kill" is a scripted fake-out.

26. **The Grandmist Serpent (world boss)** — Server-wide world boss that spawns on a fixed clock and scales its HP by the number of participants in range, giving level-20 players a shared open-world target without instance matchmaking.
27. **Ancient Stone Guardian Prime (world boss)** — A world-scale remix of the Stone Guardian whose slam telegraphs now ripple across the whole 200x200 unit zone edge, teaching the entire zone to respect arena borders rather than a single boss ring.
28. **Wandering Talisman Giant (world boss)** — A giant that patrols the zone border and only becomes attackable when enough players carry an Elder Lin-completed talisman, tying world-boss access back to the 4-step main quest instead of a portal click.
29. **Seasonal Harvest Koi (world boss)** — A time-limited koi world boss that drops jade (wallet currency) directly, giving non-crafters a clean weekly jade sink-and-source loop without monetization.
30. **Paper Crane Flock Leader (world boss)** — A world boss that summons a flock of paper cranes which must be thinned before the leader lands, turning the world-boss pull into a two-stage open-field skirmish.
31. **World-Boss Contribution Tiers (world boss)** — Award tiered credit by damage, healing, and dodge-rolls-performed so a Physician who sustains a tank earns the same loot slot as a Sword Cultivator who only DPS'd, keeping world bosses fair across the 3 classes.
32. **World-Boss Spawn Cadence Heralds (world boss)** — Precede every world boss with a 5-minute mist-herald warning (rolling fog, receding mountains darkening) so the encounter is a scheduled event the whole zone can gather for, not a surprise one-shot.

33. **Health-Threshold Phase Morphs** — Extend the Stone Guardian's 3-phase pattern to every new boss by swapping the boss's silhouette and one telegraphed attack at 66% and 33% HP, giving each fight a readable escalation instead of a static moveset.
34. **Arena Transformation Between Phases** — Have the Ink Caverns Brush Warden physically redraw the arena geometry (walls, gaps, safe platforms) each phase, so the terrain itself is the phase timer and players re-learn footing rather than re-learn a rotation.
35. **Phase-Gated Add Waves** — Tie add spawns to phase thresholds rather than a fixed clock, so a high-damage group that rushes a phase inherits the adds from the skipped window, punishing burst without ever reading a hidden enrage.
36. **Phase-Specific Debuff Overlays** — Apply a phase debuff (e.g. paper-cut bleed in phase 2) that a Physician must cleanse with a class skill, making the healer's role change by phase instead of being a flat throughput check.
37. **Ink-Blackout Phase** — Add a recurring blackout phase where the arena goes to ink (#1a1a1f) except for the boss's vermilion telegraph, so high-contrast assist mode and the paper-dark (#d9cdb0) safe floor become the actual mechanic.
38. **Qi-Drain Phase** — Add a phase where the boss drains 20 Qi per second from all players in a ring, forcing the group to bank Qi before the phase so the dodge roll (15 Qi) remains available for the phase's dodge checks.
39. **Phase Reset of Telegraph Order** — When a boss crosses a phase, reshuffle the order of its cone/line/beam sequence so muscle memory from the previous phase is the thing that kills players, not new damage numbers.
40. **Phase Transition Procedural Cue** — Give every phase change a distinct procedural audio/visual cue (brush stroke sound, palette shift from jade to vermilion) so color-assist and reduce-motion players get the same phase signal the sighted player sees.

41. **Rotating Beam Sweep** — Give a boss a rotating beam telegraph whose sweep direction reverses after each full rotation, so players read the reversal from the ink-outline arrow rather than a fixed clockwise pattern.
42. **Arena Bullet Spiral** — Add a radial bullet spiral that fires 36 projectiles from the boss center in a blooming fan, testing gap-timing with the dodge roll's 1.5s cooldown as the only invulnerability window.
43. **Timed Ground Rune Rings** — Draw expanding ground runes that detonate in order, so the safe play is stepping into a just-detonated rune (the classic "follow the blast" dodge) rather than out of everything.
44. **Cone Breath Telegraph** — Standardize a cone breath that charges with a visible ink-fill progress bar, letting players judge the exact release frame and dodge-roll at the fill peak instead of guessing.
45. **Line Dash Telegraph** — Add a line-dash telegraph whose path is drawn the full length first, then the boss dashes after a fixed 0.6s, making pre-positioning perpendicular to the line the dominant dodge strategy.
46. **Cross-Hair Targeted Circle** — Mark one player with a targeting circle that follows them for 2s before a pillar detonates at their last position, so the marked player baits the blast into a wall rather than the group.
47. **Ring Expansion (Doughnut) Telegraph** — Add a ring that expands outward with a safe inner donut, forcing players to move inward toward the boss (then out) — the inverse of the Stone Guardian's outward slam, kept visually distinct with a gold (#c9a227) rim.
48. **Checkerboard Floor Pattern** — A floor that alternates detonating squares in a checkered sequence, so players dodge onto the just-turned-safe color; high-contrast assist mode sharpens the two tones to meet the palette.
49. **Orbital Projectiles** — Give a boss orbiting projectiles that rotate around it and fire one at a time toward the nearest player, making the tank-orbit direction a positional call for the whole party.
50. **Ink-Outline Flash Telegraph** — Before every lethal attack, flash the boss's ink outline to full brightness for 0.3s as a photosensitive-safe (single low-luminance pulse) universal warning that works even for players ignoring colored zones.
51. **Bouncing Projectile Pattern** — Add projectiles that bounce once off arena walls, so the safe zone is the reflection-free corridor — teaching geometry reads that deepen every later bullet-hell phase.
52. **Screen-Edge Brush Rain** — An arena-wide attack that rains brush strokes from the screen edge, leaving a brief safe lane at the last-painted row; the lane is announced through an ARIA live region "safe lane left" for screen-reader parity.

53. **Ramping Soft-Enrage Aura** — Give bosses a soft-enrage aura that ticks up 5% damage every 10s past a 3-minute mark, so a slow-but-safe group feels pressure to finish without a hard wipe wall.
54. **Hard Enrage Wipe** — Add a hard enrage that converts the boss's next telegraph into a full-arena ink fill, an explicit wipe whose countdown is read aloud so no group dies to a hidden timer.
55. **Enrage-as-Burn-Phase** — Convert enrage into a burn phase where the boss stops casting and only auto-attacks at +150% speed, rewarding the group for holding cooldowns and a Physician cooldown for the final sprint.
56. **Qi-Denial Enrage** — Make enrage drain the group's Qi to zero and lock regen, removing dodge rolls entirely so the last 10% becomes a pure positioning fight — a soft-enrage that removes safety rather than adding damage.

57. **Adds That Buff The Boss** — Spawn adds that channel a stacking damage buff onto the boss until killed, so single-target tunnel-vision on the boss is directly punished through a visible buff counter on the boss bar.
58. **Interrupt-Gated Adds** — Add caster adds that must be interrupted within a window or they self-destruct on the party, giving the Sword Cultivator a real interrupt priority and the Talisman Master a ranged interrupt fallback.
59. **Totem/Seal Summons** — Have a boss drop vermilion seal totems that pulse a zone-wide effect until destroyed, creating secondary objectives that split the party between boss damage and totem clearing.
60. **Mirror-Image Summons** — Summon mirror images that mimic one of the boss's telegraphed attacks on a delay, so the group must read which image carries the real (full-ink-outline) version.
61. **Adds That Drop Qi Orbs** — Make certain adds drop Qi orbs on death that refund 20 Qi, turning add-clear phases into a deliberate Qi-recovery window so the group can chain dodge rolls into the next phase.

62. **Rarity-Weighted Boss Loot** — Weight boss loot by the 4 rarities (common/uncommon/rare/epic) with a fixed per-boss rare floor, so a specific boss always guarantees at least one rare and never floods the 30-bag inventory with commons.
63. **Boss-Specific Set Pieces** — Give each dungeon and raid boss a signature gear piece among the 6 slots (e.g. Brush Warden's ink-tipped blade for the Sword Cultivator) so a full boss set is a concrete 6-boss chase, not a random stat roll.
64. **Bad-Luck Protection Counter** — Track a per-boss pity counter that guarantees a rare after N kills without one, surfaced on the paper-scroll loot UI so players see the drip-feed progression and never walk away empty-handed.
65. **Crafting-Recipe Boss Drops** — Make each dungeon boss drop one of the 10 crafting recipes as a rare, so the crafting loop and the boss loop interlock instead of recipes living only in the shop.

66. **Weekly Raid Lockout** — Lock the Celestial Brush Dragon raid to one loot roll per character per week with a paper-seal stamp on the UI marking completion, resetting on a fixed server weekly cadence.
67. **Dungeon Daily vs Weekly Split** — Give the three dungeons a daily reset for the normal tier and a weekly reset for the heroic tier, so dungeons are a daily loop while heroic loot stays scarce.
68. **World-Boss Personal Weekly Cap** — Cap world-boss loot at one reward per week per character while still allowing participation for contribution tiers, so open-world bosses don't become a farmable jade faucet.
69. **Lockout Paper-Scroll UI** — Render each boss's lockout state as a wax-seal icon that stamps "sealed" after completion, giving players an at-a-glance weekly board that matches the paper-scroll UI rather than a text table.

70. **Group-Size Scaling** — Scale boss HP and add counts by party size (1-8) with smooth breakpoints, so the same Stone Guardian teaches a solo player and a full party without either feeling tuned against them.
71. **Level-Scaled Regional Bosses** — Scale regional boss HP and damage to the highest-level player in the encounter within the 20-level cap, so a level-8 and level-20 player can fight together without the boss being trivial or lethal to the lower level.
72. **Difficulty Tiers (Normal/Heroic/Mythic)** — Add a Normal/Heroic/Mythic selector per dungeon that ramps telegraph speed and adds one new mechanic per tier, reusing the same procedural boss art for three difficulty grades at zero new-asset cost.
73. **Telegraph-Speed Scaling** — Scale telegraph wind-up time with difficulty instead of only scaling HP, so "harder" means faster reads of the same ink-outline tell, which is exactly the skill the game's dodge-roll combat is built around.

74. **Solo Story-Mode Boss** — Add a solo-tuned story version of each dungeon boss with reduced HP and a single mandatory dodge mechanic, so the 4-step Elder Lin chain and side quests can showcase a boss without a group gate.
75. **Group-Only Raid Mechanics** — Keep raid mechanics (nine-image split, multi-seal activation) genuinely impossible solo, so raids read as coordinated content and not just bigger HP bars.
76. **Party-Size Scaling Slider** — Let a party leader pick an intended group size before a dungeon so scaling is predictable for a 3-player party, avoiding a hard "1-4" or "5-8" cliff in the scaling formula.

77. **First-Kill Server Announcement** — Broadcast a server-wide first-kill of each dungeon and raid boss with the player's title in the notice stream, giving the first kill social weight beyond the loot itself.
78. **First-Kill Merit Seals and Titles** — Award a permanent merit seal and a brush-painted title to the first group that downs each raid boss, recorded on the atlas page so the achievement is a durable, inspectable trophy.
79. **No-Damage Achievement** — Add a "flawless" achievement for defeating a boss without taking a hit, tracked by dodge-roll usage and hit registration, rewarding mastery of the telegraphed-read combat over gear score.
80. **Speed-Kill and Flawless Hooks** — Expose speed-kill (under X seconds) and no-death flags as achievement hooks that also grant a small jade bonus, so achievement hunting has a wallet-visible reward without touching monetization.
