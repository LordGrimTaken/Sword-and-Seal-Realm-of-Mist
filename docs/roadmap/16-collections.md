# Collections & Achievements

1. **Bestiary: 27-Mob Ledger** — A journal tab that records a brush-ink entry for each of the valley's 27 mobs the first time you slay one, so Bamboo Spirits, Fox Spirits, Jade Serpents, Lotus Toad Wardens, and Stone Hounds each get a stamped page with level range and drop table.

2. **Bestiary: Rare-Mob Spotlight** — Give Old Whisker, the Grey Fox a vermilion-trimmed bestiary page that unlocks only after witnessing his server-wide defeat announcement, recording his 20-minute respawn and Silver Tail Cloak drop.

3. **Bestiary: Guardian Phases** — The Stone Guardian's bestiary entry splits into three phase seals (slam, cone/line telegraph, rotating beam) that fill as you survive each phase, so completion tracks how deep a solo run actually got before enrage.

4. **Bestiary: Poison Notes** — A Jade Serpent entry that documents its 0.55s lunge windup and 4-second poison, teaching Physicians that Purifying Light and Lotus Antidote both clear the bite.

5. **Bestiary: Telegraph Primer** — A first-kill Bamboo Spirit page that diagrams the cone/line telegraph shapes, turning the bestiary into the tutorial for dodging ground tells before the Stone Guardian.

6. **Bestiary: Drop Table Reveal** — Bestiary pages reveal loot tables only after N kills (e.g., 10 Bamboo Spirits), so players earn knowledge of Fox Tail, Jade Scale, and Hound Fang sources rather than reading a wiki.

7. **Bestiary: Kill Counters** — Each bestiary page shows a lifetime kill tally alongside the existing achievement counters, giving a single place to see "Bamboo Bane 60/60" progress without opening the merit journal.

8. **Bestiary: Weakness Hints** — Mob entries add one tactical line per class (Sword Cultivator closes on Lotus Toad Wardens, Talisman Master kites Stone Hounds, Physician cleanses poison), written for this game's three kits.

9. **Codex: Material Compendium** — A crafting codex that unlocks entries for Bamboo Fiber, Fox Tail, Jade Scale, Serpent Gall, Toad Jelly, and Hound Fang as they enter your 30-slot bag, each showing which of Tao's 10 recipes consumes it.

10. **Codex: Recipe Scroll Library** — A collection of all 10 Artisan Tao recipes as discovered scrolls, with class-specific weapon variants (Houndfang Jian / Brush / Needles) shown per the class that unlocks them.

11. **Codex: Consumable Index** — A two-entry index for Healing Tonic and Lotus Antidote that notes heal percentages and the antidote's cure, so new players learn the only two combat consumables exist and when to spend coin at Peddler Hao.

12. **Codex: Gear Gallery** — A paper-scroll gallery that records every gear piece you have equipped or crafted across the six slots (weapon, head, body, boots, charm, pendant), marking each with its Common/Fine/Rare/Epic rarity diamond pips.

13. **Codex: Keepsake Shelf** — A shelf collecting one-of-a-kind quest keepsakes — the Paper Lantern, Guardian's Seal, and Cloudmist Pendant — so story mementos aren't lost to bag pressure and can be reviewed after the chain ends.

14. **Atlas: Six-Site Fog** — An atlas page listing all six places of interest (Hermit's Rest, Old Waystone, Serpent's Rest, Fox Fire Hollow, Hound Ridge Cairn, Cloudmist Overlook) that fills in only as you discover them, tying the existing Cartographer seal to a visible map.

15. **Atlas: Undiscovered Silhouettes** — Undiscovered POIs render as faint mist-grey mountain silhouettes on the round minimap rather than blank space, so exploration feels like completing an ink-wash panorama instead of ticking a hidden list.

16. **Atlas: Lore Reread** — Once discovered, each atlas entry preserves its one-line lore (the mask on the Old Waystone, the warm tea at Hermit's Rest) so players can revisit the Hollow Sect breadcrumbs after the discovery toast fades.

17. **Achievement: Dodge Adept** — A merit seal for negating 50 hits with the 0.25s dodge-roll i-frames (15 Qi, 1.5s cd), tracking the Dodge event the server already emits and rewarding mechanical mastery across all three classes.

18. **Achievement: Qi Disciplinarian** — Earn a seal for finishing the Stone Guardian fight without ever letting your 100-point Qi pool hit zero, teaching players to respect the 10/s regen and 15-Qi dodge cost under pressure.

19. **Achievement: Phase Survivor** — A seal for reaching the Stone Guardian's enrage phase and surviving 30 more seconds, gating a high-tier combat achievement behind the boss's hardest telegraph pattern.

20. **Achievement: Poison Purged** — Survive 20 Jade Serpent poison applications while only curing with Lotus Antidote (never letting poison run its full 4s), reinforcing the antidote's cure-window design.

21. **Achievement: Pack Breaker Solo** — Defeat an entire Stone Hound pack of three without another player dealing damage to any hound, a solo seal that respects the pack-pull aggro radius.

22. **Achievement: Toad Pacifist's Revenge** — Kill 10 Lotus Toad Wardens that were never struck before their line-spit windup, rewarding the patient pull-then-sidestep pattern of the passive mob.

23. **Achievement: No-Damage Bamboo** — Clear 5 Bamboo Spirits in Elder Lin's first quest while taking zero damage, a speed/timing seal for the tutorial quest that doubles as a mechanical check.

24. **Achievement: Class Mastery Triad** — Reach level 20 as each of the three classes (Sword Cultivator, Talisman Master, Physician) on separate characters, an account-wide seal that honors the lv20 cap and all three kits.

25. **Achievement: Rotation Perfect** — Land one full hotbar rotation (all four skills, keys 1-4) without a gap against a single mob for each class, cementing the "cast everything on cooldown" Qi math in a checkable feat.

26. **Achievement: All Skills Trained** — A seal for using all 12 skills across the three classes at least once (4 skills each), an account-wide collection of the full skill catalog.

27. **Achievement: Jade Economy** — Accumulate 100 jade from boss kills, Elder Lin chain completion, and daily board rewards only, tracking the rare currency's three legitimate sources.

28. **Achievement: Coin Fortune** — Hold 5,000 coin at once without spending at Peddler Hao or Tao's crafting coin fees, a hoarding seal for the soft-currency economy.

29. **Achievement: Crafting Apprentice-to-Master** — Extend the existing Apprentice Smith (craft 5) into a tiered seal at 5, 25, and all-10-recipes, completing the crafting collection arc.

30. **Achievement: Full Wardrobe** — A seal for having equipped every one of the 6 slots with a Rare-or-better item simultaneously, building on Well Dressed and A Rare Find into a pinnacle gearing check.

31. **Achievement: Daily Streak** — Complete every daily board task for 7 consecutive in-game days, tracking the six rotating tasks (Bamboo Purge, Fox Tails, Pond Patrol, Shrine Duty, A Word with Mei, Steady Cultivation) as a calendar.

32. **Achievement: Quest Log Clear** — Finish all 8 side quests plus Elder Lin's 4-step chain, mirroring the Friend of the Plaza seal but as an explicit "story complete" marker for completionists.

33. **Achievement: First Discovery** — A seal for being the first player on a server to discover a POI (the server records the timestamp), a light competitive twist on the otherwise personal atlas.

34. **Achievement: Deathless Valley** — Reach level 20 without dying once, a hardcore-optional seal tracked by the death state the server already owns, for players who master dodge and heal timing.

35. **Achievement: Whisker's Bane Solo** — Defeat Old Whisker alone (no ally damage), a prestige seal for the ~5x-health named rare with the wide bite arc.

36. **Achievement: Guardian No-Slam** — Defeat the Stone Guardian while dodging every telegraphed slam (zero slam hits taken), a boss-perfection seal that validates the "leave the glowing ring" tutorial line.

37. **Titles: Seal Mender Display** — Surface the existing Seal Mender and Keeper of the Gate titles in a dedicated title rack so quest-earned honorifics are visible and selectable next to achievement titles.

38. **Titles: Selectable Prefix** — Let players equip one earned title (e.g., "Cartographer" or "Hero of the Valley") to render after their name in chat and above their head, driven by the prog message's title/titles fields.

39. **Titles: Title Rarity Trim** — Render titles with a subtle rarity trim (common ink, epic vermilion) so Master of the Valley (epic) reads visually distinct from Fox Hunter (rare) on the paper scroll.

40. **Titles: Class Honorifics** — Add per-class title variants for reaching level 20 (a Sword Cultivator honorific distinct from the Physician's), so the same achievement yields a class-flavored name.

41. **Titles: Rare-Hunt Epithets** — Award unique titles for rare feats, such as "Whisker's End" for soloing Old Whisker (distinct from the Whisker's Bane kill title), expanding the epithet pool beyond quest rewards.

42. **Titles: Secret Title Hooks** — Hide one or two titles behind undocumented counters (e.g., discover all 6 POIs before level 5) so the valley holds secrets for explorers to discover.

43. **Titles: Title Gating by Merit Points** — Award a "Distinguished" title at a cumulative merit-point threshold (e.g., 150 points) so titles also reflect total achievement score, not just single feats.

44. **Titles: Preview Unearned** — Show locked titles as faint ink silhouettes in the rack with their unlock condition, so completionists can see what remains without datamining the server's ACHIEVEMENTS list.

45. **Transmog: Wardrobe Slot Override** — A costume system that lets you wear one gear piece's stats while displaying another's appearance, stored per-slot (weapon, head, body, boots, charm, pendant) so Silver Tail Cloak's look can ride on a stronger robe.

46. **Transmog: Rarity Lock** — Only allow transmog between pieces of equal-or-lower rarity, so an Epic Cloudpiercer Jian look cannot be faked onto a Common sword, preserving rarity as a visible signal.

47. **Transmog: Crafted Skin Unlock** — Crafting a piece with Tao permanently unlocks its appearance as a skin, tying the 10-recipe crafting loop to cosmetic collection and giving crafters a reason to make every item.

48. **Transmog: Procedural Palette Tints** — Offer ink/paper/vermilion/jade/gold re-tints of a gear appearance once collected, keeping every transmog inside the Ink and Vermilion palette with no neon.

49. **Transmog: Class Weapon Integrity** — Restrict weapon skins to the same class's weapon type (Jian for Sword Cultivator, Brush for Talisman Master, Needle case for Physician) so a Physician never appears to wield a jian.

50. **Transmog: Set Bonus Previews** — Show whether a full appearance set (e.g., all Wanderer's pieces) is collected, with a visual set-complete mark, encouraging collection of matched sets beyond raw stats.

51. **Transmog: Paper-Scroll Preview** — Render transmog previews on a paper-scroll doll with the existing gear-slot layout, reusing the gear UI so the costume tab feels native to the journal.

52. **Transmog: Motion-Safe Preview** — The costume preview never spins or animates when reduce-motion is on, honoring the accessibility rules while still showing static ink-outlined models.

53. **Mounts: Cloudmist Crane** — A crane mount (matching the ambient cranes) earned from the Cartographer seal, a no-combat speed boost so exploration collection yields a traversal reward without breaking combat balance.

54. **Mounts: Bamboo Spirit Steed** — A Bamboo Spirit companion mount unlocked by the Bamboo Bane title (60 kills), thematically echoing the mob you farmed rather than a generic horse.

55. **Mounts: Mount Disable in Combat** — Mounts auto-dismount on taking or dealing damage, so the 20Hz combat loop, Qi costs, and dodge roll are never bypassed by mount speed.

56. **Mounts: Pet as Non-Combat Follower** — A small Fox Spirit cub pet that follows the player outside combat, purely cosmetic with no aggro, damage, or pathing into boss telegraphs.

57. **Mounts/Pets: Collection Roster** — A roster page listing every collected mount and pet with the achievement that earned it, so companions form a completionist set like the bestiary.

58. **Pets: Pond Koi Companion** — A koi pet earned from Pond Purifier (25 Jade Serpents) that swims in a small mist ring at your feet, reusing the pond koi art and echoing the poison fights that earned it.

59. **Pets: Whisker Cub** — A rare silver-fox cub pet dropped by Old Whisker at a low rate alongside the Silver Tail Cloak, giving the 20-minute-respawn rare a chase cosmetic beyond its cloak.

60. **Pets: Pet Nameplate Off** — Pets carry no nameplate, ARIA label, or screen-reader noise by default, so they never compete with the chat/loot ARIA live regions for assistive-tech focus.

61. **Seasonal: Lantern Festival Collection** — A time-boxed collection of festival lanterns scattered at POIs during a spring event, turning the atlas into a seasonal scavenger hunt with a limited-time seal.

62. **Seasonal: Frostfall Ink Set** — A winter set of snow-tinted transmog skins (Frost Seal-themed for Talisman Masters) collectible only during a winter window, reusing the existing snowflake pictogram.

63. **Seasonal: Serpent Bloom Season** — A summer event where Jade Serpents drop a limited "Bloom Scale" variant collectible, deepening the pond-farm loop during the season without new mobs.

64. **Seasonal: Guardian Rematch Badge** — A recurring seasonal challenge to defeat the Stone Guardian under a modifier (e.g., enrage always on), awarding a dated seasonal seal each rotation.

65. **Seasonal: Rotation Calendar** — Show active seasonal collections on Fen's notice board as dated scroll entries, so players see what is live, what ends soon, and what is retired.

66. **Seasonal: Non-Grind Guarantee** — Seasonal collectibles are earnable through normal kills/POIs/quests, never gated behind jade or payments, honoring the no-payments scope and keeping seasons accessible.

67. **Seasonal: Retroactive Catch-Up** — Allow missed seasonal cosmetics to be re-earned in a later season's catch-up event, so completionists aren't permanently locked out by a missed window.

68. **Seasonal: Season Title Log** — Keep a permanent log of earned seasonal seals with their season name and year, so the collection doubles as a personal history of the valley's events.

69. **Completionist: Grand Seal** — A capstone "Valley Sage" title and seal awarded only when every bestiary entry, atlas POI, achievement, and recipe is complete, the single completionist pinnacle for the slice.

70. **Completionist: Percentage Tracker** — A journal progress bar summing bestiary, atlas, achievements, titles, transmog, mounts, and seasonal collections into one ink-bar percentage, giving a concrete completion number.

71. **Completionist: Missing-Piece Hints** — For the last 5% of completion, show faint "nearby undone" hints (e.g., "one POI still hidden in the north"), so the final stretch guides rather than frustrates.

72. **Completionist: Repeatable Prestige** — After the Grand Seal, unlock a prestige counter that re-tracks kills/POIs toward a cosmetic gold-brush flourish on the paper scroll, rewarding continued play without new power.

73. **Completionist: Server-First Notices** — Broadcast a valley-wide system message when the first player reaches the Grand Seal, using the same sysAll channel as the Old Whisker rare announcement.

74. **Completionist: Merit Points Leaderboard** — A read-only merit-point ranking on Fen's board, using the existing merit seal points as the score, so completionism has a social dimension without PvP.

75. **Account-Wide: Shared Merit Seals** — Make achievement seals and titles account-wide so earning "Guardian Breaker" on a Sword Cultivator also honors your Physician alt, matching the account-wide intent already implied by class-mastery seals.

76. **Account-Wide: Per-Character Bestiary Progress** — Keep bestiary kill counters per-character (they track that character's combat history) while making the drop-table/phase knowledge unlocks account-wide, so alts inherit revealed info but not inflated counts.

77. **Account-Wide: Shared Transmog & Companions** — Unlock transmog skins, mounts, and pets account-wide once earned, so cosmetics and companions follow you across the three classes you level.

78. **Account-Wide: Shared Atlas** — Make the atlas fog-of-war and POI discoveries account-wide, so re-exploring all six sites is not re-grinded per character.

79. **Account-Wide: Identity in Launcher Token** — Tie account-wide collections to the existing name+password/launcher token identity (not yet full accounts), so shared progress keys off the current login credential without introducing a separate account system.

80. **Account-Wide: Cross-Character Preview** — Let the journal show which character earned each account-wide item (a small "Earned by [name], Sword Cultivator" line), so shared collections still tell each character's story.
