New to the valley

Your first hour

A route through the opening hour that ends with gear, coin, two skills used properly and a boss dead. Nothing here is a prerequisite — the valley does not push.

  1. Sign in and choose a path

    Create an account in the launcher, then pick a character name and a class on the in-game scroll. Sword Cultivator is the most forgiving first pick; Physician if you expect to play with friends; Talisman Master and Blade Dancer reward positioning more than reflexes.

  2. Learn the two buttons that matter

    Space rolls and makes you briefly untouchable, and every skill costs Qi from the bar under your health. Practise rolling out of the plaza's bamboo spirits before your first real fight.

  3. Follow Elder Lin

    He stands by the gate and hands out the opening chain. It teaches targeting, the hotbar and turning in, and it pays enough coin and gear to make the first boss survivable.

  4. Collect the side work

    The zone guide lists who asks for what. Lantern Keeper Mei's errand and the daily notice board at the plaza are the cheapest experience in the game; the daily board resets at midnight UTC.

  5. Dress for the boss

    Take every drop with a green arrow, then visit Artisan Tao with Hound Fang or Bamboo Fiber to craft the set piece you are missing. Set bonuses — two pieces, three pieces — matter more than one high-level item.

  6. Take the shrine

    The Stone Guardian waits at the northern shrine. Every big attack paints the ground first: leave the ring, do not tank it. Below a quarter health it adds a sweeping beam, so save a dodge. After that, press J and enjoy the journal entry.

Accounts and passwords

How do I create an account?

Open the launcher and choose to create an account. You need an account name and a password. Your account name is separate from your character names, which you pick later on the in-game login scroll.

I forgot my password

We cannot read your password. It is stored only as a salted hash, so it can be reset but never recovered.

Is my password safe?

Passwords are hashed on the server and are never stored in plain text. The launcher exchanges your sign-in for a one-time token; the game window never sees your password. Use a password you do not use elsewhere.

Can I delete my account and data?

Yes. Email us from your account address and we will delete the account and its characters. See the privacy policy for what is stored.

Launcher problems

Windows says "Windows protected your PC"

Preview builds are not code-signed yet. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Details and checksum steps are on the download page.

macOS says the developer cannot be verified

Control-click the launcher, choose Open, and confirm. Or use System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway.

The Linux AppImage does not start

Make it executable (chmod +x) and install libfuse2. If you run it from a terminal, copy any error text into your bug report.

The download or update stalls or fails

The launcher resumes where it stopped, so close it and open it again first. Every file is checked against a SHA-256 checksum, and a damaged file is fetched again. If it keeps failing, check your disk space and any antivirus or proxy software that rewrites downloads.

"Launcher update required"

The game needs a newer launcher than you have. Download the latest from the download page and install it over the old one.

The game window is black or runs slowly

Turn on hardware acceleration and update your graphics drivers. In the game, press F1 to open help and settings, where a performance mode renders at lower resolution.

Network and ports

Which ports and servers does it use?

Everything goes over outbound HTTPS on port 443: downloads from dl.swordandseal.com, the account service and secure WebSocket game connection on play.swordandseal.com. You do not need to open or forward any ports at home.

It cannot connect on my school or work network

Some networks block WebSocket traffic or unknown domains. Try another network or ask the network owner to allow the two domains above. The status badge at the top of every page shows whether the servers are up.

The badge says "Servers offline"

The badge asks the game server for a heartbeat every minute. If it says offline, the servers may be down for maintenance, or your network is blocking the request. Check the news page for announcements.

How do I beat the Stone Guardian?

Bring friends and a Physician, and watch the ground. Every big attack paints a ring first. Leave the ring before it lands. See the zone guide.

Reporting a bug

Email {{cfg.supportEmail}} with as much of this as you can:

  • What you did, what you expected and what happened instead.
  • Your operating system and the launcher version (the version number is also on the download page).
  • Your account name and character name. Never send your password.
  • The time it happened, with your time zone.
  • A screenshot, and any error text.
