World

Cloudmist Valley

Begin in a lantern-lit plaza under a paifang gate. Cross the stone bridge over the Lotus Pond, hunt bamboo spirits and fox spirits, and climb to the ruined shrine where a Stone Guardian still keeps watch.

The valley is a single 200 by 200 unit zone, but it is dense: levels 1 to 20 are all playable here, from the safe plaza out to the far corners of the map.

What is in the game today

A small world, built properly

This is a playable prototype, not a mock-up. Everything below is running in the current build.

  • Server-authoritative multiplayer

    One persistent valley on a 20 Hz authoritative server. Your client predicts, the server decides, and other players share the same zone, chat and boss.

  • Three classes, four skills each

    Sword Cultivator, Talisman Master and Physician, with twelve hand-drawn brush pictograms on the hotbar, cooldowns and ranges.

  • Boss fights with telegraphed attacks

    The Stone Guardian marks the ground before it strikes. Read the glowing rings, step out in time, and heal through the second phase.

  • Daily notice board

    Notice Board Keeper Fen posts three tasks a day from a rotating pool: slay, gather, talk or cultivate for coin, jade and experience.

  • Brush-ink visuals

    Cel-shaded ink outlines, layered misty mountains, paper-scroll interface and vermilion seal stamps. No neon, no generic fantasy.

  • Quests, levels 1 to 20

    Elder Lin's four-part quest chain, Lantern Keeper Mei's errand, loot, a bag, coin and jade, and a level curve that takes about an hour and a half to cap.
