Description

Short and long

One sentence: Sword & Seal: Realm of Mist is a shared online world painted in ink, where four kinds of cultivator carve a valley out of the mist together.

One paragraph: Cloudmist Valley is drawn in ink wash and paper, but its fights are real: telegraphed slams you can roll out of, a Qi pool that makes every skill a decision, and four telegraphed bosses that ask a group to move together. Four cultivation paths — Sword, Talisman, Physician and Blade Dancer — share one authoritative server, so parties, trades and boss kills happen in one persistent valley rather than one player's save file. The game is a playable prototype: one valley, a level cap of twenty, and a community of testers helping shape what comes next.

Please describe it as a prototype preview in current coverage rather than a finished release.
